`backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/availability.py`. They are pure, take `now`-free inputs, and follow
domain-rules 1.6: the reported figure less bookings confirmed at or after the report's `effective_at`,

apply to.

## Decisions and assumptions
- No DEC entry was needed. The prompt's rules match `domain-rules.md` 1.6 and `contracts.md`.
- `confirmed_at` is a sequence of instants supplied by the caller. The functions cannot tell which
  service a confirmation belongs to. Callers (D07 status, U08 board) must pass only the confirmations for
  the report's service. This is a caller contract, not checked here.
- Naive datetimes in `confirmed_at` are not validated here. Comparing them with the aware `effective_at`
  raises `TypeError`, which fails loudly.
- Pending uses identity (`is`) with `BookingStatus.REQUESTED`, not `==`, to avoid the cross-enum equality
  in lesson L3. Covered by a test.
