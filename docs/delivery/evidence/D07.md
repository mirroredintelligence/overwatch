## Summary
`compute_status` now gives a service's status (accepting, limited, not-accepting, over-capacity, not-updated, out-of-hours), its effective available or occupied figure, used count, over-by count and freshness, from its profile, latest report, confirmed booking instants and the clock. It follows domain-rules 1.2 and 1.3 in order, with integer arithmetic and UTC instants. It did not exist before.
