## Privacy
Scan of the diff `main..HEAD` (backend and docs/delivery) for postcode, NHS and titled-name shapes. Every hit is a synthetic probe or a spec sample:
- Postcode shapes: `DT4 8XX` (spec sample), `DT48XX`, `dt48xx`, `dt4 8xx`, `QZ9 9ZZ` (invented, not a real postcode; `SW1A 1AA` was removed in round 1 because it is a real postcode).
- NHS-number shapes: `943 476 5919`, `9434765919`, `943-476-5919`, `943 476-5919`. The D17 prompt gives `943 476 5919` as its sample; it is the number NHS documentation uses as an example. It is not checked against any record system and is used only as a string the guard must flag.
- Titled names: `Mrs Smith`, `Dr Jones`, `Dr. Jones`, `Miss Jones`, `Mr. Brown`. All invented, used as probes.
- No real patient, name, NHS number or postcode appears in a fixture, template or log.

**Shapes the scan covers (exactly):**
1. Full UK postcode: one or two letters, a digit, an optional letter or digit, a digit, two letters. Case-insensitive, word-bounded, any whitespace run or none between the halves.
2. NHS-number shape: 10 contiguous digits, or 3-3-4 with a space or hyphen at each join (the joins may differ), not part of a longer digit run.
3. Personal title Mr, Mrs, Ms, Miss or Dr, in any case, optional full stop, whitespace, then a capitalised word.
4. Forbidden call-level field names: name, patient_name, nhs_number, postcode, address, dob, date_of_birth, free_text (refused by `render`).

**Applied where:** every value a caller passes to `render` (lists and tuples searched inside), and the rendered text, subject and HTML.

**Shapes not covered:** a bare two-word name with no title (`Jane Smith`); a lowercase name with no title; NHS-shaped numbers split by letters or other punctuation; free text in general; `need` as free lowercase wording.
