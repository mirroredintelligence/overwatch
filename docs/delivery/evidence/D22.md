## Decisions and assumptions
- **DEC-032** (new, on this branch): the approval gate takes booleans and a scope flag, with no actor, so it cannot check who approved. The approver-differs rule lives in the use case (`ApproveRegistration`, `ApproveCriteria`). Whether registration approval needs two distinct people is left for the owner: domain-rules 6.1 names two approvers but gives no separate rule.
- Transition check runs before the approval checks, so `approve_registration(SUBMITTED, incomplete)` raises `InvalidTransition`, not `NotAllowed`. The prompt does not specify the order. Both are `DomainError`s, so a caller gets a refusal either way.
- Missing checks use `is not True` and `is not ScopeFlag.IN_PILOT`, so anything other than the exact passing value counts as missing (fail closed).
- Dates in the invitation tests use 2026 (UTC), matching the prompt's 23 to 25 October example.
- Decision number: DEC-032 chosen after checking `decisions.md` on `main` and on every `task/*` branch (highest taken: DEC-031).
