Decision: `docs/delivery/decisions.md`, DEC-1365. Design system: `docs/design/design-system.md` sections 2, 3, 4, 6.1 and 13. Branch `claude/design-tighten-attention-dd83ef`, not merged into `main`, not pushed.

## What was checked, and how

| Claim | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Tokens carry the tightening (radii, spacing, one type scale, shadows, surfaces) | `frontend/app/tokens.test.ts` (updated deliberately: the pinned values changed, the 13-colour count and the motion tokens did not) |
| Body 14 px or more, touch targets kept (52 px buttons, 48 px chips) | `tokens.test.ts`, "the tightening pass" |
| The wall board keeps its earlier values | `tokens.test.ts` pins the `[data-surface='wall']` block; browser comparison `DEC-1365-shots/wall-before/wall-screen.png` against `after/wall-screen.png`: same size, differences confined to three bands of text, the live "Updated N min ago · next due HH:MM" lines (the clock moved between runs); no card, radius, shadow or size differs |
| Glow classes and tokens | `frontend/app/attention.test.ts` (reads the shipped `attention.css`), `tokens.test.ts` ("the glow tokens": colours, 3:1 against white and the page ground, period at least 2 s) |
| Slow, never flashing, never off | `attention.test.ts`: one `ease-in-out` cycle, opacity between 0.5 and 1, no `steps()`, period is the token |
| Reduced motion: no animation, static full-strength ring | `attention.test.ts` (the `prefers-reduced-motion` block); screenshot `after/board-reduced-motion.png` taken with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'` |
| Glow added to, never instead of, the shape and word | `Tile.attention.test.tsx`, `ServiceCard.attention.test.tsx` (the badge, `data-shape` and word are present on every glowing card) |
| A stale figure is not spare capacity, not shrunk, not hidden | The same tests (the number is "?" and the words "Phone to confirm" or "Current capacity unavailable"); `attention.test.ts` ("never changes what the card says": no size, display, order, colour or fill in any attention rule) |
| Stale wins over over-capacity and limited | `lib/attention.test.ts`; tile and card tests (a last-known, paused or old over-capacity item glows stale) |
| No ranking | `Board.attention.test.tsx` and `ServiceCard.attention.test.tsx`: the tile and card order equals the order given |
| The wall has no glow | `Tile.attention.test.tsx` (no `attention` prop, no class), `Board.attention.test.tsx` (the wall view has no `[data-attention]` or `.attention`) |
| Text contrast stays AA | The glow is outside the card; the existing pinned pairs in `components/board/styles.test.ts` and the token tests pass unchanged; the lighter tints were re-checked by `styles.test.ts` (`--c-tint` pairs 4.5:1) |

## Not verified

- The full Playwright suite was not run; only the screenshot spec. No real device, no deployed site, no assistive technology.
- The glow was judged by eye on the stand-in data. Many glowing cards at once, and a stale figure on an Accepting tile (blue halo next to a blue border), are the visual weak points recorded in DEC-1365 item B4.
- The tightening was applied by token and by a mechanical replacement of hard-coded radii and coloured button shadows in module CSS outside `components/board`; the pages not shot here (reporter, registration, practitioner, admin) were not looked at. Their tests pass.
- The wall's pairing and reconnect pages read the new tokens; they are not the board.
