# Evidence: G02 FHIR-to-graph mapper: resources, primitives, references

Builder: build-G02 · Branch: `task/G02-fhir-graph-mapper` · Prompt: `docs/plan/prompts/G02.md`

## Summary
A pure mapper turns a FHIR R4B resource (dict) into a graph fragment (one node per resource, one relationship per reference, primitives as properties) and back. It covers resources (R1), primitives and lists of primitives (R2), and references with `fhirPath`, `order` and `display` (R3). Unmapped elements raise `UnsupportedElement`. The round trip is exact or refused: an empty `meta`, and top-level `iri`, `versionId` or `lastUpdated`, are refused with `ValidationFailed` (F1 fix, DEC-049 item 7). No `json` property is written (DEC-001 overrides R1; see DEC-049).
