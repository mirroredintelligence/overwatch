- Cancel contract (item 1): no new DEC. DEC-102 already records the decision: the port contract stands, the cases are not skipped, and the adapter maps only the exact emulator refusal (`ServiceBusError`, text `SequenceNumber = N is already being cancelled`, same N) to a no-op. This keeps the port as the source of truth, so option (b) (skips) was not needed and no skip was added. This choice has not been confirmed by a live emulator run.
- The session open window is bounded to 10 s with a 5 s minimum. The value 10 s is a chosen budget, not measured.
- The SDK's own retries on a timed-out open (default `retry_total`) are not bounded. That is the likely source of the 27 s setup, but it is not confirmed.
