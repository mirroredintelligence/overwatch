# Evidence: I03 Outbox relay worker

Builder: build-I03 · Branch: `task/I03-outbox-relay` · Prompt: `docs/plan/prompts/I03.md` (its tenancy amendment applies) · Decision: DEC-157

## Summary
`OutboxRelay` reads each tenant's pending outbox rows from Neo4j, publishes them to Service Bus, and marks them published in a new unit of work, at least once. `ScheduleWork` and `CancelWork` rows become scheduled and cancelled Service Bus messages, with the sequence number stored on the `ScheduledWork` node. One relay runs at a time per tenant (cache lock `outbox-relay`, 30 s). The relay is a worker with no request, so it takes its tenants from a directory, binds one tenant per round and opens every unit of work under it; every message it sends carries `tenant_id`. The process entry is `python -m diucs.bootstrap.outbox_relay_main` (DEC-157 item 13: the layers contract keeps it out of the worker module).
