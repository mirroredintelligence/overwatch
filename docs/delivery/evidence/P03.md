## Summary
People now exist in the graph: a Practitioner (a person, with only a keyed phone hash), and a PractitionerRole (a post at a service, with its code, channel and cover). A phone lookup returns every active reporter role it holds, minus any it is covered out of at `now`, plus any it covers until after `now`. Before this, nothing stored people or posts, and no inbound text could find the services a phone reports for.

## Decisions and assumptions
All deviations and assumptions are in `docs/delivery/decisions.md` as **DEC-064** (items 1 to 16). The main ones:
- `phoneHash` and `emailDomain` are node properties written after the put, not FHIR (item 1). The FHIR form has no telecom (item 2).
- `PractitionerRoleCode` is an enum in `codes.py`, and `PractitionerRole.code` is typed as that enum. The prompt says `str` (item 4).
- `ResourceStore._load` skips relationships with no `fhirPath` (item 7). This is a change to a delivered task (G05), recorded here so the owner can see it.
- A role's period gates prompts: in effect from `period_start` to `period_end` (item 10). The rule book is silent on this, and it is an assumption.
- `services_for_phone_hash` returns the service name and sorts by service id. Query #3 says `ORDER BY s.name` (item 9).
- Mapper: one backbone entry for `Practitioner.name` (item 3). Codes: `PractitionerRoleCode` added to `codes.py`, and its expected values added to `tests/unit/domain/test_codes.py` (item 4). Extensions: `coveredBy` and `coveredUntil` registered, and the registry test updated from fifteen to seventeen names (item 5, lesson L66).
- No migration: the `Practitioner.phoneHash` index already exists (`0007_phone_hash`) (item 14).
