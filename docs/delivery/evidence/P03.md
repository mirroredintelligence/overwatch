## Summary
People are in the graph. A Practitioner holds a keyed phone hash (64 lowercase hex characters only), a bare email domain, and a staff display name that may not carry a postcode or a run of ten or more digits. A PractitionerRole is a post at a service with its code, channel, period and cover. The phone lookup returns the active reporter roles a phone holds, minus those covered out of at `now`, plus those it covers until after `now`, sorted by service name, then service id, then role id. An ambiguous phone lookup is a Conflict.
