## Decisions and assumptions
All deviations and assumptions are in `docs/delivery/decisions.md` as **DEC-064** (items 1 to 16) and **DEC-065** (items 1 to 6). Item 6 (round 3) records the owner's reading that `display_name` is a staff name, so a plain name is allowed, while a postcode or NHS-number shape is refused. The residual gap, an eleven-digit number written inside a name, is recorded there for the owner. DEC-064 items 9 and 11 are superseded by DEC-065 items 2 and 1 respectively. The main ones:
- `phoneHash` and `emailDomain` are node properties written after the put, not FHIR (DEC-064 item 1). The FHIR form has no telecom (item 2).
- `PractitionerRoleCode` is an enum in `codes.py`, and `PractitionerRole.code` is typed as that enum. The prompt says `str` (DEC-064 item 4).
- `ResourceStore._load` skips relationships with no `fhirPath` only for CHANNEL and COVERED_BY (DEC-064 item 7, narrowed by DEC-065 item 5). This is a change to a delivered task (G05).
- A role's period gates prompts: in effect from `period_start` to `period_end` (DEC-064 item 10). The rule book is silent on this, and it is an assumption.
- Phone hash and email domain are validated in the Practitioner constructor, and the phone hash again at both lookups (DEC-065 items 3 and 4).
- No migration: the `Practitioner.phoneHash` index already exists (`0007_phone_hash`) (DEC-064 item 14).
