## Decisions and assumptions
- **DEC-057** (new, on this branch): `subscribe` registers at the call, not on the first read; fencing tokens are a per-name counter that never goes down (Redis must use a non-expiring INCR key); expiry is inclusive at the end and judged in UTC; releasing a non-current lock is a silent no-op.
- `docs/spec/contracts.md` Additions `cache.py` line notes the registration rule.
- DEC number chosen after checking `decisions.md` on main and every `task/*` branch (highest was DEC-056).
- DEC-010 stub used for the red commit (see Red run).
- No TTL validation: a non-positive TTL is treated as already expired. Not a tested behaviour.
