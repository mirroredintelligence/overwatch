## Port docstring and async iteration (fix 2)
`Cache.subscribe` is typed `-> AsyncIterator[str]`, and its docstring says it returns "an iterator of messages". The port therefore requires async iteration by its type, not only `anext`. The case `test_a_subscription_can_be_read_with_async_for` pins it, and M17 (no `__aiter__`) fails it. The fake's `__aiter__` is kept, not removed.
