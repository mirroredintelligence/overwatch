## Tests that are forced rather than observed (disclosure)
Three ConstraintError mapping tests force the constraint error by replacing the query call with a monkeypatch that raises it: `test_contact_store_errors.py::test_a_constraint_error_on_put_is_a_conflict`, `test_credential_store_errors.py::test_a_constraint_error_on_set_pin_is_a_conflict`, `test_credential_register_errors.py::test_a_constraint_error_on_register_is_a_conflict`. Reason: Neo4j's MERGE and unique checks serialise real racers, so a real race does not reliably reach the mapping. The tests are deterministic, but they test the adapter's mapping, not the engine's behaviour.
