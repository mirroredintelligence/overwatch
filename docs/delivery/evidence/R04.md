## Summary
Services and capacity reports are now readable and writable over FHIR. `GET /fhir/HealthcareService` (search by `active`, `service-category`, `organization`, with `_count` and `_offset`) and `GET /fhir/HealthcareService/{id}` (with an ETag) read services; `POST /fhir/Observation` creates a report by running the `ReportCapacity` use case (so range, back-dating, flags, audit, outbox and replay are the use case's, not the route's); `GET /fhir/Observation?focus=...` returns the latest report; `GET /fhir/Observation/{id}` is where `Location` points; `PUT`, `PATCH` and `DELETE` on a report are 405. Both resources are in the CapabilityStatement. Every outgoing resource is validated as FHIR R4B.
