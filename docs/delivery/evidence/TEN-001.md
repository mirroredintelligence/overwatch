## Summary
`run_query` and a new `run_global_query` take a registered `NamedQuery` and nothing else: a string, or an object that is not the registered one, is refused before the transaction is touched. The tenant context, scope id and binding come from the archived branch (tip `2bdca21`) without its mode; the reader in `queries.py` is not reused. Registration is a cheap check (`$tenant` or the global allowlist, plus a `scenario` field) against a ratchet, `UNCONVERTED`, of the 126 existing statements and templates. An architecture test fails on any raw driver, session or transaction `run` outside the execution point, on Cypher built from pieces, and on a literal statement at a runner call; a probe module that does all of it makes the check fail.
