## Summary
The pure domain core of a tenant now exists: the `Tenant` record with its identifier, time zone and status rules, contacts, wording and configuration versions; the bounded `TenantPolicy` with `DEFAULT_POLICY` equal to today's behaviour; the 27-row setting registry with classes, scopes, approvals and the wording text rule; and the lifecycle state machine with activation blockers, the 30-day offboarding notice, the 90-day number quarantine and the purge rules. No I/O, no clock, no persistence, no Neo4j. Names are in `docs/spec/contracts.md` (new section) and the choices the prompt left open are in DEC-155.
