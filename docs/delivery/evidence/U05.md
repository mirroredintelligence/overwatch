# Evidence: U05 HandleInboundText use case: capacity replies

Builder: build-U05 · Branch: `task/U05-inbound-text` · Prompt: `docs/plan/prompts/U05.md`

## Summary
A text from a reporter's phone is now read as a capacity reply: parsed (D10), judged by the reply policy (D11), and recorded through `ReportCapacity` and `ConfirmCapacity` (a figure, a Y, a correction, a grouped reply) or refused with the right message (C5, C6, C7, C2, C11, and the new C12). An unknown phone gets no write and no reply. **Hazard H-31 is built:** a Y on a figure older than its window (D05's 2 hours, at the moment the prompt named it) calls no `ConfirmCapacity`, writes nothing, and tells the reporter the figure is out of date and asks for a number (C12). The decisions are DEC-148.
