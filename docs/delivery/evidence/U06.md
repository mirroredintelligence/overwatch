# Evidence: U06 Missed-report ladder handlers

Builder: build-U06 · Branch: `task/U06-missed-report-ladder` · Prompt: `docs/plan/prompts/U06.md` · Decision: DEC-166

## Summary
A service that does not answer its prompt is now chased and flagged. `MissedReportLadder` runs the three steps SendPrompts (U03) schedules: at +30 min a reminder (C8, fresh link, new reply-by of now + 30 min) and a `StatusChanged` to Not updated, at +60 min `PhoneTaskRaised` on `capacity.events`, at +90 min `ReportEscalated` on `alerts.events`. `record_report` (U01, reused by U02 and U05) cancels a service's scheduled ladder steps in the unit of work that stores a report timed at or after the prompt's due time, and a step that finds the prompt answered, or the service inactive or suspended, stops itself. `prompt-due` work is never touched. Two events are new in `domain/events.py`. No Cypher is added.

## Decisions and assumptions
- DEC-166 records every choice below. Deviations from the prompt: the constructor is `(uow_factory, clock, ids, link_tokens, link_base_url, sealer)` (no `idempotency`, a sealer added); no `scheduled_for_service` statement is added (item 3); a duplicate step for one missed report is `ignored` (item 8); `LadderStepResult` has `communication_id`.
- `[ASSUMED]`: `REMINDER_REPLY_WINDOW` = 30 minutes (named constant). Out of hours is not an exception to the ladder (domain-rules 1.5 gives none); inactive and suspended services are not chased.
- A step runs at `max(clock, its own time)`: a clock a second behind the bus does not word a reply-by early (item 6). There is no "not due yet" error; I05 should not retry on one.
- `_send_payload` and `_phone_hash` are imported from `send_prompts.py` (private names) so that one function seals a message and one reads a holder's phone; they were not renamed because that file is U03's.
- Events are added to `domain/events.py` and listed in `contracts.md`; two helper functions there take a field name.
