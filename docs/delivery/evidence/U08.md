## Round 2: verifier fail at 3a1c4d9 (code 5f489f2), fixed
The verifier failed the task on two points. Both are fixed here. The clock change (test 4 read at 14:25) was accepted by the verifier and is unchanged.

1. **Bulk rule.** The board made one `confirmed_since` call per service. Fixed by a batch method, `BookingRepository.confirmed_since_for_services(service_ids, since)`, added to P02's port and to contracts.md Additions (row for U08, DEC-088). It runs one named query, `Q-BOOK-CONFIRMED-FOR`, for all services. Each confirmed booking is then loaded for its `confirmed_at`, as `open_for_services` loads its bookings. `confirmed_at` lives only in the output entry (DEC-063 item 5), so it cannot be filtered in Cypher without a second copy of the fact. DEC-085 item 1 is superseded by DEC-088.

Added `test_bookings_count_only_for_their_own_service`: UCR West keeps its pending request and availability 4, and a confirmed UCR East booking takes East's last place (available 0) without changing UCR West. Also added `test_confirmed_since_for_services_gives_each_service_its_own_confirmations` (batch result per service, the `since` filter, and an empty key).
