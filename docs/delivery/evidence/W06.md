# Evidence: W06 Wall board live updates and Last known

Builder: build-W06 · Branch: `task/W06-wall-live` · Prompt: `docs/plan/prompts/W06.md` · Decisions: DEC-152, DEC-153

## Summary
The wall board now keeps itself current and says so when it cannot. `/screen` renders `LiveBoard`, which listens to the event stream (through `GET /api/bff/events`, as the paired screen's session), refetches the whole board on every event, every stream reconnect and every 60 seconds, and replaces it atomically. It shows Last known, in words, shape, time and age and never as colour alone, when the stream has been down for more than 10 seconds and the refetch failed, when an answer cannot be read, or when `generatedAt` is more than 3 minutes older than the back end's clock. This closes hazard H-33 for the wall and H-20 for the wall board.
