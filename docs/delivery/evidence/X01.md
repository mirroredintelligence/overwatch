## Derivation chosen for the change days
A London day is a clock-change day when its UTC offset at 00:00 local differs from its UTC offset at 23:59 local (`is_change_day`, computed with `ZoneInfo("Europe/London")`, not with `diucs.domain.rules.london_time`). The test `test_change_days_are_the_last_sundays_of_march_and_october_for_every_year` checks that, for every year 2020 to 2040, the derived days are exactly the last Sundays of March and October. The 2026 list (`CHANGE_DAYS`) is kept for the exhaustive and boundary tests, unchanged.
