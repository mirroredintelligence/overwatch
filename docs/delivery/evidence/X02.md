## Decisions and assumptions
- **DEC-015** (new, in `docs/delivery/decisions.md`): two conflicts in the design system.
  - `--dur-sheet` is 320 ms in the table, against the "UI motion under 300 ms" rule. Kept at 320 ms and pinned by a test.
  - Caption is 14 px in the type table, against "not below 16 px anywhere". Kept at 14 px. Owner to decide.
- Prompt test 3 says no duration above 300 ms except onboarding. Read literally, it fails on `--dur-sheet` at the table's own value. The test has `--dur-sheet` as the one stated exception.
- Reduced motion zeroes `--dur-onboard` as well as the other movement tokens (section 5.5 "zero for movement tokens only"). This also makes opacity fades in those roles instant. DEC-015 records this for the owner.
- Token names are my choice, since the prompt names only some: `--space-1` to `--space-8`, `--radius-card`, `--radius-chip`, `--type-<role>-{size,lh,weight,tracking}`, `.tabular-nums`. `--radius` stays as the control radius.
- `layout.tsx` is unchanged. Root and body sizes are set in `tokens.css`, so no layout change was needed.
- Headings `text-wrap: balance` and the dark-mode tokens are not in this task. Dark mode is out of scope per the prompt.
