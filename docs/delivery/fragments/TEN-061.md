# TEN-061: two-member network tests: grant lifecycle, leave, history, concurrency (W9A)

Tests: `tests/integration/application/test_grant_lifecycle.py` (12), `tests/integration/application/test_network_history.py` (9), with `tests/support/network_admin_rig.py` (`AdminRig`) and `tests/support/network_admin_fakes.py`. All run on the shared Neo4j (parallel mode) and passed.

## Decisions

- DEC-972 The rig drives the product, not a copy of it. `AdminRig` wires the real use cases (TEN-056, 077, 078) to the real registry, the real units of work of both kinds and a directory of the five organisations of TEN-060's world; one clock a test moves; and the two workers' halves: `deliver_events` reads each acting tenant's outbox and hands every `network.events` message to the projector's handler, `project`/`apply_pending`/`beat` run the producer in the member's scope and the apply step in the network's (the heartbeat included). So a test that asks "the second approval starts the projection" proves it through the outbox and the handler, and a leave through the use case, not through a hand-made helper. What the tests show: dual approval in either order; a grant that starts later projects nothing until then and ends with its window (no event, the heartbeat's reconciliation); end by the grantor stops that kind only and the other kinds and the other member are untouched; a projection that arrives after the end is rejected `grant-not-effective` and writes nothing; an end and a projection racing for two members leave no live row whatever the order; a member that stops reporting reads Not updated on the real board with no new event; the network scope holds no poison and every network invariant holds; a member that leaves is in the history and not the live reads, its own data untouched, the removal is the same end with its reason, a re-join is a new membership with new grants (version 2) and live rows, every chain verifies (the network's link by link, a known issue), a second leave records nothing, an invitation can be withdrawn before it is accepted.
