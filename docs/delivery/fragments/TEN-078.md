# TEN-078: network administration use cases: grants and the cross-scope saga (W9A)

Back end: `application/use_cases/grants.py` (`ProposeGrant`, `ApproveGrant`, `DeclineGrant`, `EndGrant`, `ReviseGrant`, `SupersedeDueGrants`, `QueryGrants`), `bootstrap/workers_main.py` (the supersession in the heartbeat).

## Decisions

- DEC-966 Grants. A grant is proposed for one scope, for the network's one purpose, under the agreement version in force and the projection allowlist version of the build (`PROJECTION_ALLOWLIST_VERSION`), by the grantor's `tenant-admin`; its start defaults to now and may not be earlier; a scope with an open grant (proposed, one approval, or approved) refuses a second proposal (it is revised: `ReviseGrant`); a new grant after an ended one takes the next version (`max + 1`), because the registry's key is (network, grantor, scope, version) and the history of a scope reads as one line. Two approvals, in either order, by the grantor's administrator and the owner's network administrator (two different accounts: when grantor and owner are one organisation, two people); `{"effective": true}` is the second approval, and then `GrantEffective` is published by the approving tenant (only by the approval that completed it; the earlier one publishes `GrantApproved`), which starts the grantor's projection (DEC-963). Both approvers racing is not an error: an approval that finds the status moved reads again and adds its own to the new state, up to three tries, so neither is lost (a conflict only after the third). A decline is either party's, before both approvals. An end is the grantor's alone (a `tenant-admin` of the grantor; the owner removes the member instead): the registry first (the projector's gate closes), then the network scope (`EndProjections`, dated by the registry's end), then the records and `GrantEnded`; ending an ended grant returns it unchanged with its first date. A revision is the next version with `supersedes_id`; it replaces its predecessor with no gap and no overlap (`supersede_grant` cuts the old window where the new one starts): at its second approval if it has started, otherwise when the heartbeat finds it due (`SupersedeDueGrants`, which runs for the tenant bound and publishes `GrantEnded` with the reason `superseded`, which ends nothing: the successor carries on). A grant is never effective by a stored flag: `GrantView.effective` is `is_effective` at the time of the read.
