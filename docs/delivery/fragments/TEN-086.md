# TEN-086: hazard log rows and tenancy runbooks (W9A)

Runbooks: `docs/delivery/runbooks/tenant-provisioning.md` (create, fill in, activate, create a network), `tenant-suspend-resume.md`, `tenant-offboarding.md` (start, export, complete, purge), `grant-incidents.md` (stop sharing, a leak, a stale board, two approvers, an old agreement, a failed event). The brief put them under `docs/delivery/` (not `docs/runbooks/` as the task file list did). The hazard rows below are for the tasks of the bundle W9A (TEN-056, 061, 074, 077, 078, 079); the rows of the earlier tenancy tasks (H-T, H-N) are in the log already. Severity is the clinical safety officer's (TBD); a row's "status" is what the build did.

## Decisions

- DEC-973 The runbooks are plain documents in `docs/delivery/runbooks/`, each naming the commands as they exist (`scripts/provision_tenant.py`, `scripts/tenant_lifecycle.py`, the `/network-admin` routes), the order, what to verify and what to do when a step stops; none contains a value of any tenant's data, a number, a token or a link. A runbook is the operator's text; the human-only steps (information governance and clinical safety approval, closing a Twilio subaccount, accepting what happens to backups) are named as such and are not done by the system (DEC-133). The hazard rows carry no severity.
