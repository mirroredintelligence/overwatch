# Fragment U43: planning analysis from decision snapshots

## Decisions
- DEC-392: the audit log reads for planning are bounded: Q-PLAN-WINDOW (entries of named use cases recorded in a window) and Q-PLAN-FOR-CASES (entries of one use case naming any of the given entities, distinct). Both carry $tenant and have scenarios. Cases are joined to their decision in Python, from the entries.
- DEC-393: the default cohort is age 65-79 or 80+, urgency within-2-hrs, referrer ambulance-crew or care-home; a need group (all, falls-and-frailty, iv-therapy, palliative) narrows it. The referrer comes from the case's OpenCase entry (the outcome snapshot does not hold it). A referral is a cohort case with a decision (a FilterOptions display at or before its outcome). Best fit is the first option row of the decision (board order, see H-193). Best fit unavailable: status not-accepting, over-capacity or not-updated, or number 0. Went elsewhere: unavailable best fit, destination a listed option other than the best fit. Counts under 10 are hidden; a share with base under 10 is hidden; share rounds half up. The heat grid uses the case's arrival time (London weekday, six-hour band). Declines are not cohort-filtered (the decline snapshot holds no age). Lead and exec only; one audit entry (PlanningAnalysis, action R, no entities) holds the query parameters only.
- DEC-393 also: the result holds no case id, reference, postcode or patient value.

## Hazards
- H-193: best fit is the first option in the decision snapshot. U12 writes the snapshot in board order, not in the travel order the options are sorted by, so "best fit" can be a service that is not the nearest. Trigger: planning figures used as nearest-service evidence. Fix belongs to U12 (write the sorted option order into the snapshot).

## Known issues
- U43: the best-fit rule uses board order (H-193). Trigger: a service listed first on the board that is not nearest.
- U43: the window query returns every audit entry of the two use cases in the window, bounded by time only. Trigger: a period with very many closed cases (365 days at high volume): add a per-case page before running at scale.
