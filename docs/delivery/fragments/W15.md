# W15 fragment: record outcome, SystmOne summary and call closed

## Decision

DEC-640 The close time is the BFF's own, not the back end's. `POST $close` answers the closed ServiceRequest, which carries no close time, so the BFF answers `closedAt` as the moment it recorded the close.

DEC-641 The SystmOne summary is built from the case the back end holds and shows "Not recorded in this view" for every field the back end does not send (destination, booked, data age). It is a copy of what the back end holds, not of what the advisor just chose.
1. Once the outcome is saved, the summary, the postcode and the needs are removed from the state and from the page (test: the postcode is not in the document after close).
2. An outcome named in the address (`?outcome=no-service-fits` or `out-of-area`) is preselected and shown as text.
