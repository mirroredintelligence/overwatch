# W17 fragment: shift lead page (chasing, figures getting old, covering, system now)

## Decision

DEC-650 (W17) · The shift lead's main page is at `/lead` (file `app/(lead)/lead/page.tsx`), not `app/(lead)/page.tsx` as the task list has it; the route group does not appear in the URL (the wave prompt's rule). Build one: the ladder rows say their state in words on every row, including the escalate row, which reads "Escalated to you. Done." or "Escalated to you. Not done." (the prompt's sample row has no state word; the prompt's own rule "done and not done shown by word and icon" wins).

DEC-651 (W17, every lead task) · A back-end gap is answered, not filled. The overview needs a back-end read that does not exist (the chasing ladder, the figures getting old and the covering numbers). Its BFF route answers 501 `not-built`, and the page says it could not load. It never shows "No services need chasing." for a read that failed (H-330). The same rule applies to every lead task.

DEC-659 (W17) · The phoned dialog is a seam until W14 lands: `components/advisor/PhonedDialog.tsx` is a shell with the props W14 keeps.

## Tenant pending

- None: the W17 overview has no Cypher and no tenant input. The BFF takes none from the request (DEC-130, DEC-132). The overview's back-end read, when built, must be tenant-bound by the back end like every other read.

## Assumed

- Ladder labels: "Text sent", "Reminder sent", "Advisor phone task", "Escalated to you", each followed by its state word.
- Time format is London time, 24-hour, `HH:MM`. Overdue text: "N min" under an hour, "N hr" or "N hrs" with "M min" when M is not zero.
- Refresh every 60 s; a board update refreshes at once.
- The "Getting old" card appears only when the list is not empty.
