# W21 fragment: advisor cancel and withdrawal alert (build-W6H)

## Decision

1. Acknowledging a withdrawal or a not-reached report happens on the first action the advisor takes from the alert ("See options again", "Ask a clinical advisor" or "Phone the caller"). The alert says "Phone the caller now" first, and the back end stops the shift lead's escalation on the acknowledgement (R17). The acknowledgement does not check that the caller was phoned (H-NEW-13).
2. "Cancel request" and "Cancel booking" open the same dialog. The dialog's reasons are the four of U20; its Cancel stays off until a reason is chosen. Keep closes it with no request and gives focus back to the control that opened it.
3. A cancel answered 409 (the booking moved on) or that does not reach an answer shows "This booking changed before you cancelled. Check its status." and reads the booking again, so the card shows what the back end holds.
4. The withdrawal alert shows the reason and the withdrawal time from the Task (`withdrawnAt` from `executionPeriod.end`, `withdrawReason` from the output). Not-reached shows its own reason words.
5. While a dialog or the alert is open, the card behind it is `aria-hidden`, so its controls are not reached by a screen reader or a query. This is the modal behaviour; the W14 shared dialog is not on integration/build (see known issues).
6. Cancelled bookings show "Booking cancelled" with "Choose another option", which returns to the options list through `onSeeOptions`.

## Contracts

- `lib/advisor/bookings.ts`: `BookingState` gains `withdrawnAt`, `withdrawReason`; `CANCEL_REASONS`, `CancelReason`, `withdrawReasonWording(code)`.
- `lib/advisor/bookingServer.ts`: `cancelBooking(session, id, reason, key)`, `acknowledgeBooking(session, id, key)`.
- `lib/advisor/useBooking.ts`: returns also `cancelBusy`, `cancelConflict`, `cancel(reason)`, `acknowledge()`.
- `components/advisor/CancelBookingDialog.tsx`: `CancelBookingDialog({ kind: 'booking' | 'request', serviceName, conflict?, busy?, onConfirm(reason), onKeep })`.
- `components/advisor/WithdrawalAlert.tsx`: `WithdrawalAlert({ booking, serviceName, onAcknowledge(), onSeeOptions, onAskClinical?, onPhoneCaller? })`.
- `components/advisor/BookingCard.tsx`: `onCancel` is replaced by `onCancelBooking(reason)`; adds `cancelBusy`, `cancelConflict`, `onAcknowledge`, `onSeeOptions`, `onAskClinical`, `onPhoneCaller`.
- `components/advisor/BookingPanel.tsx`: adds `onSeeOptions`, `onAskClinical`, `onPhoneCaller`.
- BFF: `POST /api/bff/bookings/[id]/cancel` `{ reason }` (roles advisor, clinical; 200 `{status}`; 409 `{code:'state-changed', status}`; 400 `{code:'invalid'}`). `POST /api/bff/bookings/[id]/acknowledge` (200 `{acknowledged:true}`).
