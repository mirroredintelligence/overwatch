## Decision
Reporter set-up, second half (W34): home screen, notifications, PIN and "All set".
1. (DEC-661) The push subscription is saved on the All-set step, after the reporter has signed in, not when Allow is pressed. The back end's push-subscription route needs a signed-in actor (`current_actor`), and set-up issues no session until the PIN is verified (`/identity/pin/verify`). Allow therefore asks the browser for permission only; the subscription and its consent wording `v1` are posted from All set. This departs from the W34 prompt's test 3 (see the test file).
2. (DEC-663) PIN: the PIN is held in the PinPad's component state, sent once to `POST /api/bff/identity/pin`, then `POST /api/bff/identity/pin/verify` signs the reporter in. The keypad shows no digits; the live region counts them. The pad clears itself after each complete entry.
3. Home screen: platform tab from the user agent; the installed app (standalone display mode) skips the guide to a Continue button.
4. Notifications copy is exactly the prompt's; a denied permission links to `/app/notifications-off` (W37).
