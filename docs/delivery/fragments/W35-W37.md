## Decision
Reporter app, everyday use and recovery (W35 to W37). Departures from the prompts are numbered.
1. (DEC-665) The app home (`/app`) is a server redirect, not a page of its own: no session goes to `/app/pin`; one service goes to its respond page (which shows "Still right?" when a figure is on record); two or more go to `/app/update` (W38). The "latest confirmation" home is therefore the respond page, not a separate view.
2. (DEC-666) The thanks page takes the recorded figure from its address (service name, unit, value, time, next due). None of it is a phone number, a token or a patient detail. The list of today's reports is read from the BFF.
3. (DEC-667) An out-of-range figure is refused once with the usual range and kept in the field; a second send of the same figure sends `confirmOutOfRange: true` (the link page's rule, L01). A back-end refusal of a second confirm (`confirmLimit`) hides the confirm button for the screen.
4. (DEC-668) The booking decision (W36) shows its outcome in place after a decision, not on a separate `done` page: a refresh shows the status the back end holds, so no `done` route is needed. The Idempotency-Key is made once per screen load.
5. (DEC-669) Locked PIN and 30-day check reuse `CodeEntry` (extracted from set-up; the set-up code step now uses it) and `PinSetup` (now takes `doneHref`). Sign out uses the existing `DELETE /api/bff/session` (W02), not a new `POST /api/bff/session/sign-out`, since the session route already clears the cookie and needs CSRF.
6. Test-only: the `PinScreen`, `RespondScreen`, `SettingsScreen`, `LockedScreen` and `PinSetup` tests raise their timeout to 20 s (full-suite load on userEvent-heavy tests).
