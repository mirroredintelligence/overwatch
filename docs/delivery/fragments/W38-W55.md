## Decision
Several services on one phone (W38) and lost phone, sign out everywhere (W55).
1. (DEC-670) Grouped update: a row is answered on its own; "Both still right (3 and 1)" posts every due service in one batch. A partial answer never confirms the others, which stay "Waiting". The thanks lines are shown in place after an answer, not on a separate page.
2. (DEC-671) The change banner counts the phone's due services by name, so UCR East reads "1 of 2" (as on the artboard). The "Both still right" label keeps the services' letter order, so it reads "3 and 1". Two orders, two rules, both stated.
3. (DEC-672) Ownership first on every service route: a service the reporter does not act for is the same 404 as an unknown one. The batch route refuses any item that is not a confirm for an owned service before the back end is asked.
4. (DEC-673) Lost phone (`/app/lost-phone`) needs no session. The browser holds the number only for the requests. The back end answers 202 for any number, and the BFF does the same. The verify answers map to three BFF codes: wrong (400), expired (410), throttled (429); a device that is refused answers 409. The old phone is signed out by the back end when the code is right.
5. (DEC-674) Sign out everywhere (`/app/sign-out-all`) lists "This phone and N other(s)" from settings, then clears this phone's session (`DELETE /api/bff/session`) only after the back end has signed the others out.
