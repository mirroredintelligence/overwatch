# W42 fragment: lead suspend, resume, remove a person, reports to check

## Decision

DEC-657 (W42) · The suspended state is not on any read the back end offers. `GET /fhir/HealthcareService/{id}` answers the registration's `active`, which is not the suspension flag (the suspension is a service flag read by U36, not in the FHIR service). So a fresh visit to the suspend page shows the suspend form, not the suspended view. The suspended view is shown from the suspend answer on the page. The answer's time is the BFF's clock at the moment the back end took the suspension. The register number is not on any read either: "Phone the service" says so rather than showing a number from anywhere else. Closing this needs one read that gives the suspension, its time, the expired count and the register number (an R13 follow-up).

DEC-658 (W42) · A flag shows its rule in words from the screen footnote, one line per rule: "The figure was out of its usual range twice.", "The figure changed many times in a short time.", "The figure differs from what advisors recorded by phone.". A reason the screen does not know is shown as "Flagged by rules.", never dropped. The screen's detail line ("Usual range 0 to 8. Sent 14:10 and 14:12 by text.") and its figure ("reported 40, twice") are not on the back end's open-flag list, so they are not shown. Out-of-range and differs-from-phoned flags offer "Accept the figure" and "Suspend service"; many-changes flags offer "Mark as checked" (both as the prompt lists them). Every flag offers "Phone to confirm", which opens the phoned dialog's seam (DEC-659).

DEC-652 (W42, see W18) · The lead's routes call only the back end's existing routes: `$suspend`, `$resume`, `GET /admin/report-flags?status=open`, `POST /admin/report-flags/{id}/$resolve`, and `GET /fhir/HealthcareService/{id}`. The admin and identity routes sit at the back end's root, beside `/fhir`, so `backendFetch` (in `lib/fhir/client.ts`) calls the origin of `DIUCS_FHIR_BASE_URL`, with the same rule that the base ends in `/fhir`.

## Tenant pending

- None in this bundle. Each back-end call uses the lead's token; the back end binds the tenant at the edge (TEN-018). The suspension's wording and its SMS are the back end's (TEN-026 wording).

## Assumed

- The suspend answer's `since` is the BFF's clock, not the back end's (the back end does not return it).
- Suspension reasons as the back end codes them: suspected-false-reports, closed-unexpectedly, contact-unreachable, other.
- Removal reasons: left-service, changed-role, lost-phone, other.
- Flag actions: accept → `accepted`, checked → `checked` (the back end's own outcomes).
- A person given to the remove form is a role id-shaped string (letters, digits, dots, hyphens, up to 64).
