# W43 fragment: phones and services, move a service, invite a reporter, track set-up

## Decision

DEC-654 (W43) · A cover "until a date" ends at 23:59:59 London time on that date, with the offset in force on that day (GMT or BST). The BFF computes it (`endOfLondonDay`) and sends the back end one instant with its offset, so the day does not depend on the server's zone. "Until I change it" sends no `until` part at all.

DEC-652 (W43, see W18) · The invitation is real: `POST /api/bff/lead/invitations` calls `$invite-reporter` (R10) with `service` as a reference, `role`, `mobile` and `suggestedChannel` (push, text or email; "App notification" is push). The answer names the invitation, the last four digits of the number and the expiry, never the number. A service outside the pilot is a 409 with the code `not-in-pilot`; the page shows the scope words, not the back end's own sentence. The move is real: `$move` (U38) with `toMobile` and optional `until`.

DEC-651 (W43) · The phones list, the set-up tracker, resend and switch-to-text have no back-end read or write yet. Their BFF routes answer 501 `not-built`, and the pages say they could not load. An empty list is never shown for them (H-330).

## Tenant pending

- None in this bundle. The invitation and the move use the lead's token; the back end binds the tenant (R10, U38). The pilot list is the tenant's own (the back end answers 409 for a service outside it).

## Assumed

- Phone: 11 digits starting 07, spaces allowed; the example in these forms is "07700 900123".
- Channel for an invitation: push is "App notification"; the back end's own check decides which are allowed.
- The move's "until a date" is the end of that London day (DEC-654).
- Role for an invitation: free text, up to 200 characters.
