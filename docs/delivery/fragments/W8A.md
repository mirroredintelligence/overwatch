## Decisions

## DEC-860 A shift lead does not work a call: the page guard and every advisor BFF route admit the advisor only (W10, closes the question in DEC-632 and known issue W7A-K8)
Status: accepted (W8A, under DEC-133).

1. The back end's case, booking and options routes admit advisor and clinical (DEC-397) and answer a lead as NotFound. The page guard admitted advisor and lead to `/advisor`, so a lead could open a call screen that could not read or save anything.
2. Decided: the guard and the back end agree on "a lead does not work a call". A lead's job is the capacity ladder, the flags and the audit (`/lead`); a call opens a case with a referrer's details, and a person who is not taking the call should not be able to open one. `CASE_ROLES` is `['advisor']` (`lib/advisor/roles.ts`); `PREFIX_ROLES` for `advisor` is `['advisor']` (`lib/auth/access.ts`); the booking BFF routes, which admitted advisor and clinical, use the same list. A clinical advisor works from `/clinical`.
3. A lead who must take a call signs in as an advisor. No back-end change was made: widening the back end to the lead would make the lead a second role that opens cases and records capacity as advisor-phoned, which DEC-292 left to the orchestrator.
4. Tests: `lib/advisor/roles.test.ts`, `lib/auth/access.test.ts`, the 403 cases in every advisor route test, `e2e/sign-in.spec.ts` (lead is refused `/advisor`).

## DEC-861 "I phoned" with "not accepting" sends no number to the back end (W14, supersedes the wire part of DEC-638)
Status: accepted (W8A).

`RecordAdvisorPhoned.phoned_figure` refuses a value with "not accepting" ("is not used when the service is not accepting") and stores 0 itself. The BFF therefore sends `status` and `case` and no `value` for it; the browser still hides the number and still sends 0 to the BFF, which ignores it. For accepting and limited the value is a whole number 0 to 9999 (the 9999 bound is the BFF's [ASSUMED] constant). Test: `app/api/bff/services/[id]/phoned/route.test.ts`.

## DEC-862 The case as the back end holds it: the urgency extension, the district, the case number, recorded scores, the key of a save (W10, W11, W12, supersedes DEC-631 item 3 and the planned-reads-as-today rule of DEC-397)
Status: accepted (W8A).

1. A save writes the `urgency` extension and a consistent `priority`; a read takes the urgency from the extension (planned stays planned) and falls back to the priority only when the extension is absent (DEC-773). A code the rules do not list reads as none, never a guess.
2. A read carries the case number (`identifier`), the open time (`authoredOn`), `closed`, `closedAt` and `outcome`. The BFF answers them in `CaseView`. The URL segment is still the case id (DEC-631); the four-digit number is shown on screens and in the summary only. `POST /api/bff/cases` still answers `{ id }` only.
3. The back end keeps the postcode district only (DEC-398), and none once the case is closed. The needs form says "On record: postcode district DT4. Type the full postcode only to change it.", starts the field empty, and sends no postcode when none is typed (the back end leaves the district as it is). `CaseInput.postcode` is `string | null`; a district on its own is refused by the BFF.
4. Scores are recorded once and then cannot change (`UpdateCase._scores`). Once the case has scores the form shows them as text ("Recorded as the referrer gave them: NEWS2 2. They cannot be changed.") and saves them as they are; the pickers show only while none is recorded. "Widen the time" repeats the recorded scores and sends no postcode.
5. The back end requires an Idempotency-Key on a PUT as well as If-Match. The browser sends a new key for each press; the BFF refuses a save with none (400) before any back-end call. A closed case shows "This call is closed." and no form. The call bar counts from the back end's open time, not from the time the page was drawn.
6. Every id route (`cases/[ref]/*`, `services/[id]/*`, `bookings/*`) is `idScoped`: a refusal is the one 404 (DEC-130 item 7). The ids are struck from `ID_ROUTES_NOT_YET_SCOPED` in `test/guards/bff-routes.test.ts`.

## DEC-863 An outcome names the service it went to for "accepted and booked" and "redirected" only; the close time is the back end's (W15, supersedes DEC-640)
Status: accepted (W8A).

1. `RecordOutcome.check_destination` refuses both outcomes without a destination service and refuses a destination on any other outcome. The form asks "Which service did the call go to?" for those two outcomes, listing the services of the options read for the case (read when the outcome is chosen, once, with "Try again" on failure), keeps Save off until one is chosen, and drops the choice when the outcome changes to one that takes none. `parseOutcomeInput` enforces the same rule in the BFF, before any call. Before this, both outcomes were refused by the back end with 400.
2. `POST $close` answers the closed ServiceRequest with `closed-at` (DEC-781). The BFF answers that time and answers 502 when the answer is not a closed case; it no longer stamps its own.
3. The closed screen and the finish heading name the case by its four-digit number. The summary read of a closed case answers 409 `closed` (the back end builds a summary only while a case is open) and the finish screen then shows "Call closed".

## DEC-864 The criteria dialog shows what the history gives an advisor: the accepted and excluded needs and the age range (W14, supersedes DEC-639 for those two rows)
Status: accepted (W8A).

The history answer for an advisor is the current version only, with `accepts`, `excludes`, `ageMin`, `ageMax` and `reviewBy`. The BFF words them with the need labels of the case form ("Fall, no injury · Frailty · Age 18+", "Suspected fracture" or "None listed"). A need code the rules do not list makes the row null and the dialog keeps "Not shown in this view. Phone the service to check." The reviewed date and the owner are not sent to an advisor and stay "not recorded". Overdue is a review date before today in London (DEC-038).

## DEC-865 A withdrawal or not-reached alert is acknowledged only by an explicit answer (W21, builds DEC-854 option (a), supersedes DEC-838 items 1 and 5)
Status: accepted (W8A); H-495 and H-496 are built and tested, left for the orchestrator to close on verification.

1. "I have phoned the caller" and "The caller is already being helped" are the only controls that send `POST /fhir/Task/{id}/$acknowledge`. "See options again", "Ask a clinical advisor" and Escape leave the alert open and the escalation running. "Phone the caller" (which acknowledged on the first action) is gone: the alert says "Phone the caller now" and the first answer is the explicit one.
2. An alert that is set aside gives way to a standing notice on the card, "{service} withdrew. This is not answered yet: phone the caller, then answer the alert.", with "Open the alert". The notice stays until an acknowledgement is taken.
3. The card treats the alert as answered only when the back end took the acknowledgement. A failed send keeps the alert open and says "The answer could not be sent. The shift lead will still be told. Try again."; pressing an answer twice quickly sends once.
4. Tests that fail on the old first-action rule: `BookingCancel.test.tsx` ("does NOT acknowledge on See options again", "... on Ask a clinical advisor", "... on Escape") and `BookingPanel.test.tsx` (the same three through the BFF, with the seen acknowledgements asserted empty).
5. The alert is the shared Dialog as an `alertdialog` with `focusFirst={false}`: focus rests on the sheet, so no answer is one keystroke from pressed.

## DEC-866 The advisor's booking dialogs are the shared Dialog of W14 (W20, W21, supersedes DEC-838 item 5)
Status: accepted (W8A).

`CancelBookingDialog` and `WithdrawalAlert` build on `components/ui/Dialog` and draw no modal of their own; a test reads their source for it. `Dialog` gained an optional `role` (`dialog` or `alertdialog`) and `focusFirst` (default true), with tests; its other behaviour is unchanged. Escape in the cancel dialog keeps the booking.

## DEC-867 The W20 BookingPanel is mounted once per own-organisation service on the options list and on the map pin card; its live read follows `board` events (W13, W20, W50, supersedes DEC-837 item 3 and the W13 note that the request button is a prop)
Status: accepted (W8A).

1. `ServiceCard` and the map's pin card mount `OptionBooking` (the panel for this case and service) for a service of the viewer's own organisation; a network service has none (DEC-648). The `onRequest` props are gone. The panel's next steps go to `/advisor/case/<id>/finish` and `.../finish#summary`; "Back to options" and "Choose another option" bring the advisor to the options heading without reading the options again (DEC-636).
2. The back end's stream sends `event: board` (routes_events.py) and never `booking.updated`. `useBooking` re-reads the booking when either event names its service; the 30 s poll stays as the fallback.
3. A service whose number the card does not hold (the back end sends none, H-530) shows "This service's phone number is not on this screen. Find it in your service directory." wherever the card would have shown a number.

## Contracts

- Routes (BFF): every advisor route is `withBff` with `roles: ['advisor']` and `idScoped: true` where it takes an id. `PUT /api/bff/cases/[ref]` needs `If-Match` and `Idempotency-Key`; the body's `postcode` is optional. `POST /api/bff/cases/[ref]/outcome` answers `{ closedAt }` from the back end and requires `destinationServiceId` for `accepted-and-booked` and `redirected` only. `GET /api/bff/cases/[ref]/summary` answers 409 `closed` for a closed case. `POST /api/bff/services/[id]/phoned` sends no `value` for `not-accepting`.
- `CaseView` gains `reference`, `closed`, `closedAt`, `outcome`; `CaseInput.postcode` is `string | null`.
- Components: `OptionBooking` (new), `ServiceCard` takes `caseId` and no `onRequest`, `OptionsList` and `OptionsMap` take no `onRequest`, `NeedsForm` takes `caseId` only, `CallClosed` takes `reference`, `WithdrawalAlert` takes `onAcknowledge: () => Promise<boolean>` and `onLeaveOpen`, `Dialog` takes `role` and `focusFirst`.
- Test support: `test/advisor-session.ts` (`advisorRequest`), `test/fixtures/backend-shapes.ts` and its generator, `e2e/support/advisor.ts` (stand-in back end routes for the call flow).

## What is now wired, and what is still a stub

Wired to the real shapes: case open, read, save, close; options (positions, travel range and source, patient point, the two flags); criteria history; "I phoned" and "They declined"; booking request, read, cancel, acknowledge; the case number and open time; the booking and change link views (checked, unchanged). The clinical dialog (W8B, merged) is opened from a booking card's alert too. Still a stub, and why: the service phone and visiting-service areas (W8A-K2, back-end fields), the booking card's recovery on reload (W8A-K1, a back-end read).
