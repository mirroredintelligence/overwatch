# W9F routes: the insight console's back end

Both routes are at the back end's ROOT (not under `/fhir`): the BFF calls them with `root: true`. Samples of every answer are made by the back end's own mappers in `frontend/test/fixtures/insight-shapes.ts` (generated by `insight-shapes.gen.py`; run it from `backend/` after a change).

## POST /telemetry/ui

Counts the browser's `ui.*` events into the tenant's daily aggregates. Keeps no event.

- Caller: any signed-in role except `platform-operator` (that one is 401 at the edge). The tenant and the role counted are the token's. No `Idempotency-Key`, no CSRF (the BFF route that calls it takes none either).
- Request: `{"events": [{"name": "ui.click", "attributes": {...}}]}`, at most 50 events. `at` and any other key of an event are ignored. Attributes are strings (at most 120 characters), numbers or booleans, at most 30 a event. Anything else is `400 invalid` (an OperationOutcome naming the location).
- An event is counted only if it passes the schema (`domain/rules/browser_insight.py`, the browser's `schema.ts` written again): `session.id` 16 to 32 hex, `release` `n.n.n`, `device.type` phone|tablet|desktop, `app.mode` browser|installed (default browser), `page.route` a route template, `element.id` / `form.id` an upper-case code, `kind` one of the twelve friction kinds, `metric` lcp|inp|cls with `reading` a finite number from 0 up. A wrong or missing attribute drops the whole event.
- Answer `200 {"accepted": 3, "dropped": 1}`, `Cache-Control: no-store`. An empty list answers `{"accepted": 0, "dropped": 0}` and touches nothing. `503 transient` when the marker secret is not configured (nothing was counted).
- Counted: clicks and submits (`ElementUsageDaily`), friction (`FrictionDaily`), page views and errors (`RouteDaily`, `ReleaseDaily`), funnel steps (`FunnelDaily`), web vitals (`MeasureDaily`).

## GET /support/insight/{tab}?range=24h|7d|30d[&device=phone|tablet|desktop]

- Caller: `support` only (any other role: the non-revealing 404, before anything is read). `tab` is `funnels`, `paths`, `friction`, `performance` or `releases` (anything else `400`, field `tab`). `range` defaults to `7d` (`24h` is yesterday and today; the counters are UTC days). `device` is optional and is refused with 400 on `releases`. `Cache-Control: no-store`.
- A group under 10 sessions has no number. No answer names a person, a session or an advisor.

### funnels

```json
{"tab": "funnels", "range": "7d", "device": null,
 "funnels": [{"name": "reporter-set-up", "label": "Reporter set-up",
   "steps": [{"name": "Set-up opened", "count": 418, "pct": 100, "suppressed": false},
             {"name": "Set-up finished", "count": null, "pct": null, "suppressed": true}],
   "split": [{"channel": "app", "pct": 27, "suppressed": false}, {"channel": "link", "pct": null, "suppressed": true}]}]}
```

Funnels in order: `reporter-set-up`, `daily-capacity-loop`, `advisor-call`, `registration`, `criteria-review` (only these five; a funnel with no rows still lists its steps, all hidden). `pct` is the share of first-step sessions that reached the step (0 to 100); `split` is the share of first-step sessions by channel (`app` or `browser`), empty when the first step is hidden.

### paths

```json
{"tab": "paths", "range": "7d", "device": null,
 "mostCommon": [{"route": "/advisor/board", "sessions": 120, "medianSeconds": null, "suppressed": false},
                {"route": "/help", "sessions": null, "medianSeconds": null, "suppressed": true}],
 "uTurns": [{"route": "/advisor/board", "sessions": 12, "medianSeconds": null, "suppressed": false}],
 "unused": null}
```

At most 10 routes each; a hidden route comes after the shown ones, in name order. `medianSeconds` is always `null` (not measured). `unused` is always `null`: not measured.

### friction

```json
{"tab": "friction", "range": "7d", "device": null,
 "rows": [{"elementId": "ADV-OPT-REQUEST-BTN", "kind": "rage", "route": null, "events": 130, "interactions": 1000, "rate": 0.13, "review": true},
          {"elementId": "PAGE", "kind": "u-turn", "route": "/advisor/board", "events": 15, "interactions": null, "rate": null, "review": false}],
 "suppressedGroups": 1}
```

`review` is true when the rate is strictly over 5%. Page-level friction has a `route`, no rate and no review flag. `suppressedGroups` counts the groups left out for having fewer than 10 sessions. Rows are ordered by rate then events.

### performance

```json
{"tab": "performance", "range": "7d", "device": null,
 "vitals": [{"measure": "Largest contentful paint", "metric": "lcp", "device": "desktop", "p75": 2500.0, "budget": 2500.0, "unit": "ms", "suppressed": false},
            {"measure": "Cumulative layout shift", "metric": "cls", "device": "desktop", "p75": null, "budget": 0.1, "unit": "score", "suppressed": true}],
 "slowestRoutes": [{"name": "/advisor/case/[ref]", "p95Ms": 6000.0}],
 "slowestQueries": [{"name": "Q-OPTIONS", "p95Ms": 190.0}],
 "promptToBoard": null}
```

One vital row per metric and device that has data. `p75` is the upper edge of the histogram bucket that holds it. `slowestRoutes` is by LCP p95 (routes with 10 sessions or more; at most 5). `slowestQueries` is the worst daily p95 of each query id (no writer yet). `promptToBoard` is always `null`: not measured.

### releases

```json
{"tab": "releases", "range": "7d",
 "compare": {"old": "1.4.2", "new": "1.4.3"},
 "measures": [{"measure": "Largest contentful paint, p75", "old": 2000.0, "new": 3000.0, "unit": "ms", "review": true}],
 "pipeline": [{"name": "messages.outbound", "active": 3, "deadLettered": 1, "oldestSeconds": 95}]}
```

`compare` is `null` (and `measures` empty) unless two releases have 10 sessions each. Units: `ms`, `score`, `percent`. `review` is the release gate (more than 10% slower at p75, or friction more than 2 points up). `pipeline` is the tenant's own outbox by topic, read now; `oldestSeconds` is the age of the oldest waiting message or `null`; any topic that is not a known name is added up as `other`.

## BFF routes (front end)

- `POST /api/bff/telemetry` (unchanged for the browser: 204, 400, 401, 403, 413) now also sends the allow-listed events to `POST /telemetry/ui` with the session's token (no tenant, no `at`), best effort.
- `GET /api/bff/console/insight/[tab]?range=24h|7d|30d[&device=phone|tablet|desktop]` (support role, 403 for any other): checks the three against their lists (400 otherwise), forwards `range` and `device` only (no device for `releases`) and returns the back end's JSON.
