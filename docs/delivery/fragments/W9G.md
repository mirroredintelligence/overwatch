# W9G: the clinical advisor's case screen: "What the advisor saw" and "Call the advisor" (clinical-safety work)

Bundle W9G (W44 follow-up to W8B; closes the two stubs W8B left, and the part of DEC-855, H-375 and H-412 that said the screen shows the case as the advisor saw it). Branch `task/build-W9G` off `integration/build` 178b50b. Back end and front end. Number blocks: DEC-1030 to DEC-1039, H-700 to H-709, migration 0197 (not used: nothing here needs a migration).

## Decisions

### DEC-1030 "What the advisor saw" is the last options display the asking advisor had of the case, at or before the moment they asked, kept with the request (W44)
Status: accepted (wave 9, bundle W9G; under DEC-133). The prompt says "what the advisor saw at 14:13 (one line per service: status, figure, data age)"; the audit already writes every FilterOptions display as one record (DEC-290, DEC-390) and the request record already had an unused `snapshot_ref`. So `RequestClinicalAdvice` now finds the last FilterOptions audit record of that case written by the asking advisor (practitioner) at or before `now` and keeps its audit id in `snapshot_ref`. A later display, and any other advisor's display, are not what this advisor saw when they asked. The advice audit entry already named `snapshot_ref` (DEC-396), so "Logged for audit: ... what you saw" is now true. `GET /clinical/requests/{id}/seen` reads that one record. Three states: `shown` (a display is kept and read), `none` (the request kept none: the advisor had not displayed options, or the request pre-dates this change; the screen says "No options display is recorded for this request", never "nothing fitted"), `unreadable` (a display is named but cannot be read: not found in the case's trail, not a FilterOptions record of this case, or a row is not valid; one bad row refuses the whole display so half a display is never read as the whole). The client sends nothing: the request carries the case reference, urgency and reason only (H-516 unchanged).

### DEC-1031 The display snapshot rows gain `over_by` and `reason`; an over-capacity row of the older shape shows no figure (H-190)
Status: accepted. `FilterOptions` rows keep their keys and gain `over_by` (how far over capacity the service was, else null) and, for a service that was not suitable, `reason`, a closed code (`not-accepting`, `out-of-hours`, `not-updated`, `suspended`, `outside-criteria`, `criteria-not-current`; never the sentence, which carries an age). Additive: the replay and the planning analysis read by key and are unchanged. A row written before this change has neither: an over-capacity service then shows no figure (its `number` is the used count, DEC-391, which would read as free places), and a not-suitable service is explained from its status where the status says it, else only "Not suitable". The replay's own H-190 text ("+2 not shown") is not changed here; the fix for new displays is in the snapshot and the replay can now use it (known issue).

### DEC-1032 The figure and the reason are capacity-derived, with these suppression rules
Status: accepted. A figure is what the service could take (accepting, limited), "+n" for how far over (over capacity, n at least 1), and nothing for every other status (not accepting, not updated, out of hours): a stale or absent figure is never shown as a number. A bool, a negative or a non-integer is no figure. A figure older than the stale limit (over 2 hours, never at exactly two hours) is marked `stale` and the screen says "Over 2 hrs old" in words. A service never reported has the age "no time given". The service is named by its current name; a service no longer listed is "A service that is no longer listed", never its id. No postcode, district, service id, criteria id, person id, audit id or reference is in the answer. Why a service was not suitable is a closed phrase ("Outside its criteria" is the only one that depends on the case; it says no more than the age band and needs on the same screen), and "none of the services fitted" is said only when a display was read and none of its rows was an option.

### DEC-1033 Who reads: the clinician who holds the request; everyone else reads the one 404
Status: accepted. Both reads are for a clinical advisor and are a 404 for every other role (13 roles tested), another clinician, the advisor who asked, a waiting request (nobody holds it), a request whose 10-minute lock has lapsed (it is back in the queue), an unknown id and another tenant's id; the 404 body is the unknown id's. Holding is the same rule the queue uses: locked to this clinician and the lock not lapsed (`return_if_idle`). What the advisor saw may also be read by the clinician who advised the request (to review it, as the Done tab does); the contact is for a request still being worked and is a 404 once it is advised. The advisor who asked does not read either: they know what they saw and the contact is for the clinician.

### DEC-1034 "Call the advisor": the advisor's work phone, else the tenant's advisor line, else it says there is none (DEC-856)
Status: accepted. `GET /clinical/requests/{id}/advisor-contact` answers `{available, via, phone, name}`. (1) The advisor's work phone, if the tenant holds one for an active advisor: a `ProtectedContact` under the key `work-<practitioner id>` (`diucs.domain.model.people.work_contact_key`), returned whole with the advisor's name. A person who has left (inactive) is not phoned on the number they had. (2) Else the tenant's advisor line: a new tenant contact kind `advisor-line` (a desk or switchboard number, never a person, the same validation as the coordination phone), returned with no name. (3) Else `available: false` and every other field null; the screen says there is no number held and no advisor line set. The number is in this answer only: not in a URL, a log, an audit entry or telemetry. Opening it writes one audit entry (`ReadAdvisorContact`, the request id, and only `via`: work-phone, advisor-line or none) so who looked is recorded. This adds no new store of numbers (DEC-856 item 4): it reads the existing ProtectedContact store under a new key pattern. The advisor line is never in the public profile or the board (only the coordination phone is).

### DEC-1035 The screen asks for the number when the button is pressed, keeps it in memory only and removes it at send
Status: accepted. The number is not read when the page opens (so a clinician who does not need it is not shown it and the back end's audit entry means a real look). It is held in the component's state only, removed when the advice is sent, and never put in storage, the address, a cookie or a log; the BFF route logs nothing. The number is a phone link (as the coordination number is, DEC-888). A failed read says "The number could not be read. Try again."; a refusal says "You no longer hold this request, so the advisor’s number is not shown."
 
### DEC-1036 A failed "what the advisor saw" read does not stop the advice form, but says so and offers the call
Status: accepted. DEC-855 gates the form on the case read (the case on screen), and it still does. What the advisor saw is context for the advice and not the case; a clinician who cannot read it is told ("What the advisor saw could not be read. Phone the advisor to ask.") and the call to the advisor is offered. The form is not hidden for it because the same failure on a busy shift would stop every advice; the owner may tighten this (known issue) by adding the display to the form's precondition.

## Contracts

- Back end (new, at the root like the other clinical routes, `routes_clinical.py`): `GET /clinical/requests/{id}/seen`, `GET /clinical/requests/{id}/advisor-contact` (clinical role; the holder only; otherwise the one 404). Shapes in `W9G-routes.md`.
- Use cases (`application/use_cases/clinical_context.py`): `ReadWhatAdvisorSaw`, `ReadAdvisorContact`, `WhatAdvisorSaw`, `SeenLine`, `AdvisorContact`, pure `figure_text`, `why_text`, `seen_lines`, `pinned_display`; constants `SEEN_SHOWN`, `SEEN_NONE`, `SEEN_UNREADABLE`, `NO_NAME`, `CONTACT_USE_CASE`. Mapping: `clinical_mapping.seen_json`, `contact_json`.
- Changed: `RequestClinicalAdvice` keeps `snapshot_ref`; `filter_options.reason_code` and the snapshot rows (`over_by`, `reason`); `ContactKind.ADVISOR_LINE` (`advisor-line`, a phone kind); `people.WORK_CONTACT_PREFIX`, `work_contact_key`.
- Registered statements: none added or changed (the reads use `Q-AUDIT-FOR-ENTITY`, `Q-CONTACT-GET`, the tenant contacts list and the practitioner read), so `test_tenant_isolation.py` is not affected.
- Front end: BFF `GET /api/bff/clinical/requests/[id]/seen` -> `{seen}`, `GET /api/bff/clinical/requests/[id]/advisor-contact` -> `{contact}`; `lib/clinical/seen.ts` (`seenFromJson`, `seenLineText`, `advisorContactFromJson`), `lib/clinical/clinicalServer.ts` (`readSeen`, `readAdvisorContact`); `CaseView` takes `{requestId, request, seen}`; the case page reads `seen` for a held or advised request.
- Test support: `test/msw/handlers/clinical.ts` serves `/clinical/requests/:id/seen` and `/advisor-contact` at the back end's root and the BFF's contact route for components.
