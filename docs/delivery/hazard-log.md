# Hazard log

A living record of what could go wrong clinically or for data protection, what controls the design puts in place, and the test that proves each control. It is the working draft for the clinical safety officer's DCB0129 hazard log. It is not a substitute for it.

Rules for sessions:
- A task that touches a hazard's control adds the test reference to **Verified by** in the same commit. A control with no test is an open finding.
- A new hazard found while building is added at once with the next `H-nn`, and a lesson is published.
- **Severity and likelihood are assigned by the clinical safety officer.** Leave them as `TBD`. Do not guess. **Exception, build waves 4 to 7:** the hazards folded from those waves (H-225 to H-523) carry a provisional severity set by the orchestrator under DEC-133, conservatively, on the consequence before controls: **HIGH** a wrong, stale or missing clinical decision or safety escalation, patient-linked data or another tenant's data exposed, or authority a person or component should not have (a forged token, a standing cross-tenant access); **MEDIUM** a degraded clinical or operational workflow, non-patient personal or organisational data exposed, or a control whose failure another control contains; **LOW** tests, tooling, wording or cosmetics with no route to a person. The clinical safety officer's own rating replaces it. Rows from earlier waves are still `TBD`. A row marked OPEN has a decision in `decisions.md` that is not accepted.
- Source ids `F1` to `F48` are the risk codes on the user-journey boards in the design canvas (`docs/spec/ui-screens.md`).
