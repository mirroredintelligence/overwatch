The rules of the morph

A screen that changes shape for the task, and never by surprise

Change follows the person
A layout changes only when someone chooses something. Data arriving never moves a block, and the system never guesses what is wanted next.
Anchors stay put
Rail, top bar, call bar, role chip and the pinned patient summary never move, so the person always knows where they are.
Live figures are fixed
Numbers, status words and their colours do not animate for style. A block can resize around a figure, never the figure itself.
Every state has an address
Each layout is a URL, so Back, links and a reload land in the same place. Nothing is lost when the screen changes.
Motion, by how often it happens
Step to stepColumns and rows ease over about 0.5 s. Content settles with a small spring. Open a rowHeight eases, the content springs in last. One open at a time. Keyboard actionNo animation at all. Typing, Enter and arrow keys are instant. Reduced motionThe same states appear at once. Nothing is lost, only the movement.
Phone: the same four states On a phone, columns become steps and sheets. The summary becomes one line, and a card becomes a bottom sheet.
