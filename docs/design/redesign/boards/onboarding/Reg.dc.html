Why we ask
Your ODS code lets us check your organisation against the NHS register. A real person, with a work email or phone, is who the shift lead can ring back.
Why we ask
The address gives us the position on the map, so advisors see how far you are from a patient. A visiting service tells us the area it covers instead.
Why we ask
Advisors only see patients you can take. A review date makes advisors see a flag when your criteria are out of date, so nobody relies on old rules.
