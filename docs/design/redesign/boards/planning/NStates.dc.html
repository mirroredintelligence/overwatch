The rule
A degraded state never hides the scope header, never shows an old figure as current, and never falls back silently. Figures are removed only when the person may no longer see them.
A paused listing is one tile: ◌ Not updated, "?", "Phone to confirm", "Not available on the network. Phone to confirm."
