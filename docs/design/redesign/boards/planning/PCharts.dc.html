How a chart is drawn

Honest, plain and always with its numbers

Axes start at zeroBars never use a cut axis. A line may, and then says so.
Every mark is labelledThe number sits on the mark. Nothing is read off a grid.
Colour is never aloneDifferences are also pattern, position or a word. Blue and orange, never red against green.
A table beside every chartOne control switches to the table. Screen readers get the table.
Small groups are hiddenA count under 10 reads "under 10", never zero.
No chart libraryCSS and inline SVG are enough for bars, lines, grids, funnels and waterfalls.
