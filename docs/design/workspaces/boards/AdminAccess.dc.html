Why flags
Lead, exec and owner are now one role, Manager. The three rights stay apart as flags, so an executive still cannot remove a person, and a service lead still cannot change phone settings.
Careful
Saving a mapping changes what people see at their next sign-in. Each change asks for a confirm step and is written to the audit record.
