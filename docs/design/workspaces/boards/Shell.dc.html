No-scroll rules
1. Every screen fits 1440 by 900, or 390 by 844 on a phone.
2. Lists show five at a time: "1–5 of 17", Previous, Next.
3. Two clicks from home at most. Back returns to the same page of the same list.
4. One primary action per screen.
5. Status is shape plus word: ✓ ◐ ■ ▲ ○.
6. Anchors do not move. Only the body changes, and only when the person chooses.
