**Goal.** Decide whether a service is accepting referrals at a given moment, in London local time, including overnight and all-day services and clock changes.

**Read first.**
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/model/service.py` (D01): `AvailableTime`.
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/london_time.py` (F06): `LONDON`, `to_london`, `london_to_utc`.
- `docs/spec/domain-rules.md`, sections 1.1 and 1.3 (rule 2).

**Rules you need.**
- `available_times` are London local. A window `start`–`end` with `start < end` covers `start <= t < end` on the listed days.
- A window with `end < start` is overnight: it starts on a listed day at `start` and runs to `end` the next morning. So 20:00–08:00 on Friday covers Friday 20:00 to Saturday 07:59.
- `all_day` covers the whole of each listed day.
- A service is in hours if any of its windows covers the moment. Outside hours its status is Out of hours (D07 applies that; you only answer in or out).

**Build.** `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/opening_hours.py`:
- `is_in_hours(times: Sequence[AvailableTime], now: datetime) -> bool`.
- `next_opening(times: Sequence[AvailableTime], now: datetime) -> datetime | None`: the next moment strictly after `now` when a window starts, searched over the next 8 days, as UTC. Returns `None` when there are no windows, or when every day is all day (it never opens because it never closes).
- Both reject naive datetimes with `ValueError`.

**Tests to write first.** UCR West: Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00. 2026-10-23 is a Friday.
1. Fri 2026-10-23 13:12Z (14:12 BST) → in hours.
2. Fri 06:59Z (07:59 BST) → out; 07:00Z (08:00 BST) → in.
3. Fri 19:00Z (20:00 BST) → out (end is exclusive).
4. Sat 2026-10-24 12:00Z → out. `next_opening` → Mon 2026-10-26 08:00Z (08:00 GMT after the clock change).
5. Every-day 08:00–20:00 service across the change: Sun 2026-10-25 07:30Z (07:30 GMT) → out; 08:00Z → in. Sun 2026-03-29 07:30Z (08:30 BST) → in.
6. Overnight ward, every day 20:00–08:00: Sat 2026-10-24 01:40Z (02:40 BST) → in; 12:00Z → out.
7. Overnight on Friday only, 20:00–08:00: Sat 2026-10-24 06:00Z (07:00 BST) → in (the window started Friday); Sun 2026-10-25 06:00Z → out.
8. All day, every day → always in; `next_opening` → `None`.
9. Two windows (Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00 and Sat 09:00–13:00): Sat 2026-10-24 09:30Z (10:30 BST) → in.
10. `next_opening` on Fri 2026-10-23 05:00Z → Fri 07:00Z (08:00 BST).
