**Goal.** Compute when each service's capacity report is due, when the reply window closes, and when each step of the missed-report ladder fires.

**Read first.**
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/model/service.py` (D01), `rules/opening_hours.py` (D03), `rules/london_time.py` (F06).
- `docs/spec/domain-rules.md`, sections 1.4 and 1.5.

**Rules you need.**
- `every-2-hrs`: due at the opening time and every 2 hours after it, up to and including the closing time. 08:00–20:00 → 08:00, 10:00, 12:00, 14:00, 16:00, 18:00, 20:00. [ASSUMED alignment]
- `twice-a-day`: at the opening time and at 14:00 [ASSUMED]. If the service opens at or after 14:00, or closes at or before 14:00, only the opening time.
- `once-a-day`: at the opening time.
- All-day services use 00:00 as the opening: `every-2-hrs` → 00:00, 02:00 … 22:00; `twice-a-day` → 00:00 and 14:00.
- Overnight windows: due times start at the window start and may run past midnight; they belong to the day the window starts.
- Reply-by = due + 30 minutes. Ladder from the due time: prompt +0, reminder +30 min, advisor phone task +60 min, escalate to the shift lead +90 min.
- Times are London local; across a clock change the local time is kept, so UTC shifts. Return UTC.

**Build.** `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/schedule.py`:
- `REPLY_WINDOW = timedelta(minutes=30)`, `REMINDER_AFTER = timedelta(minutes=30)`, `PHONE_TASK_AFTER = timedelta(minutes=60)`, `ESCALATE_AFTER = timedelta(minutes=90)`, `TWICE_A_DAY_SECOND = time(14, 0)`.
- `due_times_on(profile, london_day: date) -> list[datetime]`: sorted, no duplicates, empty when no window starts that day.
- `next_due(profile, now) -> datetime | None`: earliest due strictly after `now`, searching 8 days.
- `current_due(profile, now) -> datetime | None`: latest due at or before `now`, looking at today's and yesterday's lists.
- `reply_by(due) -> datetime`.
- `LadderStep` StrEnum: `PROMPT = "prompt"`, `REMINDER = "reminder"`, `PHONE_TASK = "phone-task"`, `ESCALATE = "escalate"`; `ladder_times(due) -> dict[LadderStep, datetime]`.

**Tests to write first.** UCR West, Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00, every-2-hrs.

3. Saturday 2026-10-24 → `[]`.
4. Twice a day 08:00–20:00 → 08:00 and 14:00 local. Opening at 15:00 → only 15:00. Once a day → 08:00 only.
