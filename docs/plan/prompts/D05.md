**Goal.** Decide whether a service's figure is overdue, how old it is, and how to say its age in words.

**Read first.**
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/schedule.py` (D04): `current_due`, `reply_by`.
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/model/service.py` (D01).
- `docs/spec/domain-rules.md`, section 1.5.

**Rules you need.**
- The prompt for a due time is sent at that due time, and the reply-by is due + 30 minutes. A due time is **missed** when no report has arrived at or after it and its reply-by is before `now`.
- A service is **overdue** when it has at least one missed due time since its last report (look back up to 8 days of due times). With no report ever, any missed due time counts.
- Once overdue it stays overdue until a new report arrives; a later due whose reply-by has not yet passed does not clear it.
- `was_due` is the latest missed due time (set only when overdue). Board example: last report 10:00 BST, now 14:10 BST, dues every 2 hours → 12:00 was missed, 14:00's reply-by has not passed, so overdue with `was_due` 12:00 ("Not updated for 4 hrs 10 min · was due 12:00").
- Stale warning: a figure more than 2 hours old gets "Data is over 2 hrs old. Consider phoning." even when not overdue. Exactly 2 hours is not stale.
- Data-age text: under 1 minute "just now"; under 60 minutes "N min ago" (including "1 min ago"); otherwise hours and minutes: "1 hr 5 min ago", "2 hrs 10 min ago", "3 hrs ago", "1 hr ago". Minutes are rounded down.

**Build.** `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/freshness.py`:
- `STALE_WARNING_AFTER = timedelta(hours=2)`.
- `Freshness(overdue: bool, age: timedelta | None, stale_warning: bool, was_due: datetime | None)`.
- `assess_freshness(profile, last_report_at: datetime | None, now) -> Freshness`.
- `data_age_text(age: timedelta) -> str` (`ValueError` for a negative age).
