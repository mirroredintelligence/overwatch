**Goal.** Compute a service's status from its profile, its latest report, its confirmed bookings and the clock. The board, the advisor options and the alerts all depend on this rule.

**Read first.**
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/model/service.py` (D01), `model/capacity.py` (D02).
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/opening_hours.py` (D03), `freshness.py` (D05), `availability.py` (D06).
- `docs/spec/domain-rules.md`, sections 1.2 and 1.3.

**Rules you need.** Evaluated in this order; the first match wins:
1. `profile.suspended` → `not-updated`.
2. Not in hours → `out-of-hours`.
3. No latest report, or freshness says overdue → `not-updated`.
4. `occupied-patients`: occupied = `effective_occupied`. occupied > established → `over-capacity` with `over_by = occupied − established`. occupied == established → `not-accepting`, unless `can_exceed_established`, then `limited`. `occupied × 100 ≥ limited_level_percent × established` → `limited`. Otherwise `accepting`.
5. Free-count measures: available = `effective_available`. 0 → `not-accepting` (this covers "we are closed"). used = established − available (not below 0). `used × 100 ≥ limited_level_percent × established` → `limited` [ASSUMED formula]. Otherwise `accepting`.
- Use integer arithmetic, no floats.
- Numbers are filled in whenever a latest report exists, even for suspended, out-of-hours or not-updated services (the board shows "Last reported 3 beds free"). With no report they are `None`.

**Build.** `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/status.py`:
- `ServiceState(status: Status, effective_available: int | None, used: int | None, over_by: int | None, freshness: Freshness)`.
- `compute_status(profile, latest: CapacityReport | None, confirmed_at: Sequence[datetime], now) -> ServiceState`.
