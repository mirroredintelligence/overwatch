**Goal.** Summarise how much pressure the whole urgent-care system is under, for the wall-board banner, the COO and ICB home screen and the system-pressure alert.

**Read first.**
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/status.py` (D07) and `backend/src/diucs/domain/codes.py` (F07): `Status`.
- `docs/spec/domain-rules.md`, section 1.7.

**Rules you need.**
- "Limited or worse" means status `limited`, `not-accepting` or `over-capacity`. `not-updated` is unknown, not worse, so it does not count. `out-of-hours` services are left out entirely.
- Pressure: fewer than 3 limited-or-worse → `normal` [ASSUMED]; 3 or 4 → `watch`; 5 or more → `high` (also the alert threshold).
- The board banner "System under pressure" shows when every service that is in hours is limited or worse. An in-hours `not-updated` service stops the banner. With no in-hours services there is no banner.
- Example from the exec screen: "Watch · 3 of 9 services limited or worse".

**Build.** `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/pressure.py`:
- `Pressure` StrEnum: `NORMAL = "normal"`, `WATCH = "watch"`, `HIGH = "high"`.
- Constants `WATCH_FROM = 3` [ASSUMED], `HIGH_FROM = 5`, `LIMITED_OR_WORSE: frozenset[Status]`.
- `limited_or_worse_count(statuses: Iterable[Status]) -> int` (extra public name).
- `system_pressure(statuses: Iterable[Status]) -> Pressure`.
- `all_limited_or_worse(statuses: Iterable[Status]) -> bool`.

**Tests to write first.**
1. Sample board (accepting ×4, limited ×2, not-accepting ×1, over-capacity ×1, not-updated ×1) → count 4, `watch`, banner false.
2. Two limited, rest accepting → `normal`.
3. Exactly three → `watch`; exactly five → `high`.
4. Surge board (limited ×4, not-accepting ×3, over-capacity ×2) → `high`, banner true.
5. Surge board plus one `out-of-hours` → still banner true; plus one `not-updated` → banner false.
6. Overnight board (out-of-hours ×6, accepting ×2, limited ×1) → `normal`, banner false.
7. All out of hours → `normal`, banner false. Empty list → `normal`, banner false.
8. Five `not-updated` → `normal` (unknown does not raise pressure).
