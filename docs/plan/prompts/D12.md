**Goal.** Spot capacity reports that a shift lead should check by phone, using fixed rules about the reports themselves, never about how fast a person works.

**Read first.**
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/model/capacity.py` (D02), `model/service.py` (D01).
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/codes.py` (F07): `ReportFlagReason`, `ReportSource`.
- `docs/spec/domain-rules.md`, section 2.3. `docs/spec/ui-screens.md`, artboard "SuspendLeaver", screen 7 "Reports to check".

**Rules you need.**
- `many-changes`: 3 or more reports for the same service whose `effective_at` falls within 5 minutes [ASSUMED] ending at the new report's time, counting the new report. Confirmations (Y) count. Canvas example: "Ward A: 3 reports in 4 minutes. All Y, then 9, then 2."
- `differs-from-phoned`: the new report did not come from an advisor phoning, and an advisor-phoned report for the same service in the previous 60 minutes [ASSUMED] has a different value.
- `out-of-range-twice` is decided by the reply policy (D11) on the text path, because the first out-of-range number is never recorded. This function does not produce it.
- Ignore reports for other services and reports later than the new one. Each reason at most once, in the order `many-changes`, `differs-from-phoned`.
- A flag is its own record; reports are never edited.

**Build.** `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/report_flags.py`:
- `MANY_CHANGES_WINDOW = timedelta(minutes=5)`, `MANY_CHANGES_COUNT = 3`, `PHONED_WINDOW = timedelta(minutes=60)`.
- `flags_for_report(new: CapacityReport, recent: Sequence[CapacityReport], profile: ServiceProfile) -> list[ReportFlagReason]`. `recent` may or may not contain `new`; do not double-count it (match on `id`).

**Tests to write first.** Ward A, times on 2026-10-23.
1. Single report, nothing recent → `[]`.
2. Reports at 13:10, 13:12 and new at 13:14 → `[MANY_CHANGES]`.
3. Reports at 13:08, 13:12 and new at 13:14 (first is 6 minutes before) → `[]`.
4. Exactly 5 minutes (13:09, 13:12, new 13:14) → `[MANY_CHANGES]`.
5. Advisor-phoned 3 at 12:30, new text report 5 at 13:14 → `[DIFFERS_FROM_PHONED]`; same value 3 → `[]`; phoned at 12:13 (61 minutes before) → `[]`.
6. The new report is itself advisor-phoned and differs from an earlier phoned one → no `differs-from-phoned`.
7. Both rules at once → `[MANY_CHANGES, DIFFERS_FROM_PHONED]`.
8. Reports for another service in the window are ignored; a report later than `new` is ignored.
9. `new` included in `recent` is counted once.
