**Goal.** Encode the criteria version workflow (draft, approval, current, superseded) and the review-date rules that drive reminders and the "criteria not reviewed" flag.

**Read first.**
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/model/criteria.py` (D20): `ServiceCriteria`.
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/codes.py`: `CriteriaStatus`.
- `docs/spec/domain-rules.md` section 4.2.

**Rules you need.**
- Allowed transitions: `draft → pending-approval`; `pending-approval → current`; `pending-approval → draft` (sent back); `current → superseded`; any status except `retired` → `retired`. Everything else raises `InvalidTransition`.
- A change needs a second approver. Until approval, advisors keep the current version. On approval the new version becomes current and the old one becomes superseded, never deleted. The new version's number is the old one plus 1 and it records `supersedes_id`.
- Confirming criteria are still right sets a new review-by date 3, 6 or 12 months from the confirmation date (clamp to the last day of the month, e.g. 31 Aug + 6 months = 28 Feb, or 29 Feb in a leap year).
- A reminder is due from 14 days before review-by up to and including review-by [ASSUMED 14 days]. After review-by passes, the review is overdue.

**Build.** `domain/rules/criteria_workflow.py`:
- `REVIEW_REMINDER_BEFORE = timedelta(days=14)`
- `transition_criteria(current: CriteriaStatus, target: CriteriaStatus) -> CriteriaStatus`
- `is_review_overdue(criteria, today: date) -> bool` (true when `today > review_by`)
- `review_reminder_due(criteria, today) -> bool`
- `next_review_by(confirmed_on: date, months: int) -> date` (months must be 3, 6 or 12, else `ValidationFailed`)
- `approve(current: ServiceCriteria | None, pending: ServiceCriteria) -> tuple[ServiceCriteria | None, ServiceCriteria]`: returns (old marked superseded or None, new marked current). Raises `InvalidTransition` if `pending` is not `pending-approval`, and `ValidationFailed` if `pending.version != current.version + 1` or `pending.supersedes_id != current.id` (when there is a current one).

**Tests to write first.**
1. Every allowed transition succeeds (parametrised); `current → draft`, `superseded → current`, `retired → retired` raise.
2. Review by 12 Dec 2026: on 27 Nov → reminder not due; 28 Nov → due; 12 Dec → due and not overdue; 13 Dec → overdue.
3. `next_review_by(date(2026, 8, 31), 6) == date(2027, 2, 28)`; `next_review_by(date(2027, 8, 31), 6) == date(2028, 2, 29)`; months 4 raises.
4. `approve(v3 current, v4 pending supersedes v3)` → v3 superseded, v4 current.
5. `approve(v3, v5)` raises `ValidationFailed`; `approve(None, v1 pending)` → (None, v1 current).
6. `approve` with a draft raises `InvalidTransition`.
