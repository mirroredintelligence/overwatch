**Goal.** Encode alert rules for COO and ICB users and the shift lead: which status changes raise which alert, whether a subscriber is notified given their preferences and quiet hours, and when an unacknowledged alert escalates.

**Read first.**
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/status.py` (D07) and `pressure.py` (D08): `Status`, `Pressure`.
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/london_time.py` (F06): `to_london`.
- `docs/spec/domain-rules.md` section 8.

**Rules you need.**
- `service-closed`: the service moves to `not-accepting` from any other status while in hours. Planned out-of-hours closures do not count. Critical.
- `over-capacity`: the service moves to `over-capacity` from any other status. Not critical.
- `not-updated`: the service has been `not-updated` for at least the subscriber's threshold, 2 or 4 hours. Not critical.
- `system-pressure`: pressure moves to `high` (5 or more services limited or worse) from `normal` or `watch`. Critical [ASSUMED critical set].
- Quiet hours are London local, either none or 22:00–06:00 (wrapping midnight). During quiet hours with "critical only", non-critical alerts are not sent.
- Acknowledge window: 10, 15 or 30 minutes. Then the alert goes to the deputy, then to the SPOA shift lead.
- Defaults: all four kinds on, not-updated after 2 hours, no quiet hours, critical only during quiet hours, acknowledge within 15 minutes.

**Build.** `domain/rules/alerts.py`:
- `Alert`, `AlertPreferences` as in contracts.md (`AlertPreferences.__post_init__` validates `not_updated_hours in {2, 4}` and `ack_minutes in {10, 15, 30}`)
- `alerts_for_change(service_id, before, after, in_hours, now) -> list[Alert]`
- `alert_for_pressure(before, after, now) -> Alert | None`
- `not_updated_alert_due(not_updated_since: datetime, hours: int, now) -> bool`
- `should_notify(prefs, alert, now) -> bool`
- `escalation_at(alert_sent_at, prefs) -> datetime`
- `ESCALATION_LADDER = ("subscriber", "deputy", "shift-lead")`; `next_escalation_target(current: str) -> str | None`
- `default_preferences() -> AlertPreferences`

**Tests to write first.**
1. accepting → not-accepting in hours → one critical `service-closed`; out of hours → none.
2. limited → over-capacity → one `over-capacity`; over-capacity → over-capacity → none.
3. not-accepting → not-accepting → none (no repeat).
4. `alert_for_pressure(watch, high)` → critical alert; `high → high` → None.
5. Not updated since 12:00: at 13:59 with 2 hours → False; 14:00 → True; with 4 hours at 14:00 → False.
6. Quiet 22:00–06:00, critical only: an over-capacity alert at 23:30 London is not notified; service-closed at 23:30 is; over-capacity at 06:00 is. Use a date in British Summer Time and one in winter.
7. Kind not in preferences → False.
8. `escalation_at(14:12, ack 15)` → 14:27; `next_escalation_target("deputy") == "shift-lead"`; after shift-lead → None.
9. `AlertPreferences(..., ack_minutes=20)` raises `ValidationFailed`.
