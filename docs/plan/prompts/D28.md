**Goal.** Encode the rules for one phone that reports for several services: grouping prompts that fall due together, lettering them, and flagging an overloaded phone.

**Read first.**
- `backend/src/diucs/domain/rules/schedule.py` (D04): `reply_by`, `REPLY_WINDOW`.
- `docs/spec/domain-rules.md` section 2.4 and `docs/spec/ui-screens.md` artboard "MultiServicePhone".

**Rules you need.**
- When two or more of a phone's services are due within the same 10 minutes [ASSUMED], send one grouped prompt.
- Grouping: sort items by due time; start a group at the earliest; add items whose due time is less than 10 minutes after the group's first item; then start the next group.
- In a group of 2 or more, letters A, B, C… are given in service name order. A group of one has no letter. More than 26 items in a group raises `ValidationFailed`.
- A group's due time is its earliest item's due; its reply-by is `reply_by(that due)` (due + 30 minutes).
- A phone holding more than 3 services is flagged.

**Build.** `domain/rules/multi_service.py`:
- `GROUP_WINDOW = timedelta(minutes=10)`, `MAX_SERVICES_PER_PHONE = 3`
- `GroupedItem(service_id: str, name: str, due: datetime, letter: str | None)`
- `PromptGroup(items: tuple[GroupedItem, ...], due: datetime, reply_by: datetime)`
- `group_due(due: Sequence[tuple[str, str, datetime]]) -> list[PromptGroup]` (input tuples are service_id, name, due)
- `letters_for(names: Sequence[str]) -> dict[str, str]`
- `phone_overloaded(count: int) -> bool`

**Tests to write first.**
1. UCR West due 10:00 and UCR East due 10:00 → one group with `UCR East` = A and `UCR West` = B (name order), due 10:00, reply-by 10:30.
2. Due 10:00 and 10:09:59 → one group; 10:00 and 10:10 → two groups of one with no letters.
3. Three items at 10:00, 10:05, 10:12 → groups [10:00, 10:05] and [10:12].
4. Empty input → `[]`.
5. `letters_for(["UCR West", "Ward A", "Reablement"])` → Reablement A, UCR West B, Ward A C.
6. `phone_overloaded(3)` False; `phone_overloaded(4)` True.
7. 27 items due together raise `ValidationFailed`.
