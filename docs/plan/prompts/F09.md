You are a software engineer picking up one task in the `overwatch` repository (product name: DIUCS capacity). You have no memory of any earlier work. Everything you need is in this prompt and in the repository.

**The product.** DIUCS capacity helps an NHS urgent-care single point of access (SPOA) in Dorset. Community services, wards and virtual wards report how many referrals or beds they can take, by text message, a one-tap link, or an installed web app. Call advisors see a live wall board and a filtered list of services that fit a patient's needs, then ask a service to take the patient by text and wait for CONFIRMED. Shift leads chase late reports, register services and keep criteria current. Nothing anywhere holds a patient name or NHS number.

**The architecture.** Hexagonal. A Python back end (package `diucs` in `backend/src/diucs`) with four layers: `domain` (pure rules, no I/O), `application` (use cases and ports defined as `typing.Protocol`), `adapters` (inbound: FHIR REST, SMS webhook, link pages, workers; outbound: Neo4j, Redis, Azure Service Bus, SMS, email, push; `adapters/testing`: fakes for external providers only) and `bootstrap` (settings and the composition root). Dependencies point inwards only. A Next.js front end in `frontend/` talks only to its own `/api/bff/*` route handlers, which call the back end's FHIR R4B API. Neo4j is the only database, Redis is a cache, Azure Service Bus carries messages and scheduled work.

Background: `docs/spec/architecture.md`, `docs/spec/fhir-data-model.md`, `docs/spec/observability.md`, `docs/spec/ui-screens.md` (screen wording). **Goal.** Put the visual foundations in place: colour and size tokens, the Atkinson Hyperlegible font, and the one component that shows a service status as a shape, a word and a colour.

**Read first.**
- `frontend/app/layout.tsx` and `frontend/test/setup.ts` (F03).
- `docs/spec/contracts.md`, "Front end": token names and values.
- `docs/spec/ui-screens.md`, "Artboard: Main": the board legend and status chip styles.

**Rules you need.**
- Tokens (exact): `--c-primary #0B5C8C`, `--c-primary-dark #0B3F61`, `--c-limited #A34700`, `--c-closed #3A4750`, `--c-surge #6B2C91`, `--c-unknown #6B7B83`, `--c-ink #14232B`, `--c-ink-2 #26343B`, `--c-muted #44555D`, `--c-line #DDE3E6`, `--c-bg #F4F6F5`, `--c-tint #E3F1F8`, `--c-amber-tint #FFF1DC`. Also `--radius 14px`, `--btn-h 52px`, `--chip-h 48px`, `--focus 3px solid #14232B`.
- Status words: Accepting, Limited, Not accepting, Over capacity, Not updated, Out of hours, and Last known (used when the board is offline).
- Chip styles: Accepting white text on `--c-primary`; Limited white on `--c-limited`; Not accepting white on `--c-closed`; Over capacity white on `--c-surge`; Not updated and Last known: white background, `--c-ink-2` text, 2px dashed `--c-unknown` border; Out of hours: white on `--c-closed`.
- Never colour alone: each status has its own icon shape. Use: accepting a circle with a tick; limited a triangle with "!"; not accepting a circle with a bar; over capacity a diamond with "+"; not updated and last known a dashed circle with "?"; out of hours a moon. Icons are inline SVG with `aria-hidden="true"`; the word is real text.
- Text contrast must be at least 4.5:1.

**Build.**
- `frontend/app/tokens.css` imported in `layout.tsx`; font via `next/font/google`.
- `frontend/components/ui/StatusBadge.tsx` + `StatusBadge.module.css`: `type StatusCode = "accepting" | "limited" | "not-accepting" | "over-capacity" | "not-updated" | "out-of-hours" | "last-known"`; `export const STATUS_LABELS: Record<StatusCode, string>`; `<StatusBadge status={...} size?: "sm" | "lg" />` rendering `data-status` and `data-shape`.
- `frontend/lib/a11y/contrast.ts`: `contrastRatio(fg: string, bg: string): number` (WCAG formula).

**Tests to write first.**
1. For each status: renders the exact word from `STATUS_LABELS`; the SVG has `aria-hidden="true"`.
2. All seven statuses render different `data-shape` values except not updated and last known, which share the dashed shape.
3. `contrastRatio("#FFFFFF", "#A34700")` ≥ 4.5; parametrised over each chip's text/background pair, all ≥ 4.5.
4. `contrastRatio("#000000", "#FFFFFF")` ≈ 21.
5. `tokens.css` (read with `fs`) defines every token name listed above with the exact value.
6. `axe` reports no violations for a list of all badges.
