**Goal.** Add people and posts (`Practitioner`, `PractitionerRole`), the person port, and its Neo4j implementation, including the phone lookup every inbound text needs and cover arrangements.

**Rules you need.**
- A person is separate from the post. One phone that reports for three services is one Practitioner with three PractitionerRoles. When a person leaves, the post stays.
- The phone number is never in the graph. The Practitioner holds only the keyed hash `phoneHash` (the number itself lives in the contact store, P16). Practitioner FHIR has no `telecom`.
- Role codes (local code system `practitioner-role`): `duty-manager`, `coordinator`, `criteria-owner`, `clinical-lead`, `shift-lead`, `reviewer`. Reporter roles are `duty-manager` and `coordinator`.
- Cover: while `now < covered_until`, the role's prompts go to the cover practitioner and the usual holder does not count. Stored as extensions on the role (so the json round-trips) and as a local relationship `(:PractitionerRole)-[:COVERED_BY {until}]->(:Practitioner)` written by a named query after the resource store `put`, in the same transaction. Channel: extension plus `-[:CHANNEL]->(:Concept)`.
- Query #3 (`Q-PHONE-SERVICES`) extended with cover as `Q-PHONE-SERVICES-AT`: a phone gets every active reporter role it holds and is not covered at `$now`, plus every active reporter role it covers until after `$now`.

**Build.**
- `domain/model/people.py`: `Practitioner(id, active, display_name, phone_hash: str | None, email_domain: str | None = None)`; `PractitionerRole(id, practitioner_id, service_id: str | None, code: str, active: bool, channel: Channel | None, period_start, period_end=None, covered_by_practitioner_id=None, covered_until=None)`; `REPORTER_ROLE_CODES`; `role_holder(role, now) -> str`.
- Port `PersonRepository` (async): `get_practitioner`, `find_by_phone_hash` (active only), `roles_for_practitioner` (active roles they own), `services_for_phone_hash(hash, now) -> list[tuple[PractitionerRole, str]]` sorted by service id, `reporter_roles_for_service(service_id, now)`, `save_practitioner`, `save_role`.
- `graph_store/person_repository.py`: `Neo4jPersonRepository(tx, store)`, pure `practitioner_to_fhir` / `from_fhir`, `role_to_fhir` / `from_fhir`.

**Tests to write first.**
1. Unit: `role_holder` before, at and after `covered_until`.
2. Unit: `practitioner_to_fhir` has no `telecom`; round trip equal.
3. `assert_round_trip` for practitioner "p1" (hash "h1") and a duty-manager role at UCR West with channel text; `node_props("Practitioner", id="p1")["phoneHash"] == "h1"`.
4. "p1" holds roles at UCR West and UCR East → `services_for_phone_hash("h1", now)` returns both.
5. UCR East covered by "p2" ("h2") until 19:00Z: at 13:00Z "h1" sees only UCR West and "h2" sees UCR East; at 19:00Z it reverts. A `COVERED_BY` relationship with `until` exists.
6. Inactive practitioner → `find_by_phone_hash` is `None`; an inactive role is excluded.
7. A `criteria-owner` role is not returned by `services_for_phone_hash` or `reporter_roles_for_service`.
8. Saving a role again with the cover removed deletes the `COVERED_BY` relationship.
