**Rules you need.**
- A change, its audit record and its outbox messages are written in one transaction. A relay publishes later. A crash can delay a message but not lose or duplicate it. Delivery is at least once; consumers dedupe by message id.
- `(:OutboxMessage {id, topic, eventType, payloadJson, status, attempts, createdAt, publishedAt, sessionId, lastError})`, optional `-[:FOR]->` the resource. Status `pending` → `published`; `mark_failed` adds 1 to `attempts`; at `MAX_ATTEMPTS = 10` status becomes `dead` and it leaves `pending`.
- On enter, the unit of work opens one session and one explicit write transaction, creates one `ResourceStore(tx, clock)`, and builds each repository lazily from a factory `Callable[[AsyncTransaction, ResourceStore], object]`. Repositories that do not store FHIR resources ignore the store (`lambda tx, store: Neo4jOutbox(tx)`).
- Leaving the `async with` block without `commit()`, or with an exception, rolls back; the exception is re-raised. After commit or rollback the unit of work cannot be used again.
- Later tasks register new repositories by adding a factory to the mapping, without editing the class. An attribute with no factory raises `AttributeError("unit of work has no '<name>' repository registered")`.
