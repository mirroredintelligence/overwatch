**The product.** DIUCS capacity helps an NHS urgent-care single point of access (SPOA) in Dorset. Community services, wards and virtual wards report how many referrals or beds they can take, by text message, a one-tap link, or an installed web app. Call advisors see a live wall board and a filtered list of services that fit a patient's needs, then ask a service to take the patient by text and wait for CONFIRMED. Dependencies point inwards only. A Next.js front end in `frontend/` talks only to its own `/api/bff/*` route handlers, which call the back end's FHIR R4B API.
