**The platform is multi-tenant (DEC-130 to DEC-134 and DEC-136).** Each organisation is a tenant: the legal entity and data controller for its own data. The network view is read-only and capacity-derived. Patient-level data (bookings, cases, communications, reporters, audit) never crosses.
