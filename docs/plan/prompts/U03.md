**Rules you need.**
- Reply-by = due + 30 min. The link expires at reply-by, is single use, tied to this prompt and this phone.
- With a recorded figure send C1 (`service, value, unit, as_of, link, reply_by`): "DIUCS: UCR West. We have 3 referrals in the next 2 hrs (as of 08:15). Confirm or update: diucs.example/k3F9a Or reply Y, or a new number. By 10:30." With none yet send C2 (`service, unit, reply_by, link`). Channel is the profile's `channel`.
- Skip (no message) when the service is inactive, has an active `suspended` service flag (P14), or is out of hours at the due time.
- Send to `role_holder(role, now)` for each role from `reporter_roles_for_service(service_id, now)`.
- Ladder from due: reminder +30, phone task +60, escalate to lead +90 (`ladder_times(due)`). Then schedule the next `prompt-due` at `next_due(profile, due + 1 second)`.
- **Scheduling convention (used by every later task):** a scheduled job is a `ScheduledWork` node (status `scheduled`) written in the same unit of work plus an outbox message `topic="scheduled.work"`, `event_type="ScheduleWork"`, payload `{"work_id", "kind", "about_id", "enqueue_at"}`. Cancelling sets status `cancelled` and adds a `CancelWork` outbox message `{"work_id"}`. Statuses: `scheduled`, `done`, `cancelled`; handlers ignore work that is not `scheduled`. The relay (I03) calls `EventBus.schedule`, stores the sequence number, and later delivers `event_type="ScheduledWorkDue"` with `{"work_id", "kind", "about_id"}`. A use case never calls the event bus.
