**Goal.** Build the missed-report ladder handlers: when a prompt goes unanswered, send a reminder, then create an advisor phone task, then escalate to the shift lead. A report at any point stops the rest.

**Read first.**

- `application/work.py`, `graph_store/scheduled_work_repository.py` (P08).

**Rules you need.**
- Ladder from due: +30 reminder, +60 advisor phone task, +90 escalate to shift lead. From the reminder the service shows Not updated.
- Any report (number or confirmed) with `effective_at >= due` stops the ladder.
- Reminder C8 (`service, reply_by, link`) with a new reply-by of now + 30 min [ASSUMED] and a fresh link token expiring then.
- Phone task and escalation are events: `capacity.events` `PhoneTaskRaised` `{service_id, due_at, at}` and `alerts.events` `ReportEscalated` `{service_id, due_at, at}`.
- At the reminder also emit `StatusChanged` (before → not-updated) on `capacity.events`.
