**Goal.** Build `ChangeReporterNumber`: a shift lead points a service's reports at a new mobile, for cover (today, this week) or until changed. The old number stops working for that service at once.

**Read first.**

- `ports/outbound/tokens.py` (P10) `PhoneHasher`; `graph_store/contact_store.py` (P16).
- `application/use_cases/send_prompts.py` (U03), `handle_inbound_text.py` (U05).
- `application/idempotency.py` (P15); `docs/spec/fhir-data-model.md` (local relationship `COVERED_BY` with `until`); `docs/spec/ui-screens.md` "ShiftLeadFlows", "ShiftLead".

**Rules you need.**
- Duration: `today` (until 23:59 London), `this-week` (until Sunday 23:59 London) [ASSUMED], `until-changed`.
- Cover is stored as `(:PractitionerRole)-[:COVERED_BY {until}]->(:Practitioner)`. While active, prompts go to the cover phone and replies from the original phone for that service are ignored.
- `until-changed`: end the old role (`active=false`, period end now) and create a new role for the new number's practitioner (reuse one with that hash).
- Both numbers logged as hashes only. Only `lead`.
- Helper `effective_reporter_practitioner_id(role, now) -> str`.

**Build.**
- If P03's role record or repository lacks `covered_by`/`covered_until`, add them to the dataclass, the `Neo4jPersonRepository` (write and read the `COVERED_BY` relationship) and a round-trip test.
- `application/use_cases/change_reporter_number.py`: `ChangeReporterNumberCommand(idempotency_key, actor, service_id, new_mobile, duration)` → `ChangeReporterNumberResult(role_id, covered_until)`; `ChangeReporterNumber(uow_factory, clock, ids, idempotency, phone_hasher)`. Audit snapshot `{"service_id", "old_phone_hash", "new_phone_hash", "duration", "covered_until"}`.
- `SendPrompts` sends to `effective_reporter_practitioner_id`; `HandleInboundText` ignores a phone for a covered service.

**Tests to write first.** `tests/integration/application/test_change_reporter_number.py`, real Neo4j.
1. Cover "today" at 14:00 Friday → read back `covered_until` 23:59 London Friday as a UTC datetime; a 16:00 prompt goes to the new phone.
2. A reply from the old phone at 16:05 → no report, no reply.
3. At 00:01 Saturday the original phone gets prompts again.
4. `this-week` on Friday → Sunday 23:59 London.
5. `until-changed` → old role inactive, new active role for the new practitioner.
6. Invalid mobile → `ValidationFailed("new_mobile")`; advisor → `NotAllowed`; nothing written.
7. The audit snapshot holds two hashes and no number.
