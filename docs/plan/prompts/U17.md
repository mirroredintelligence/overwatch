**Goal.** Build the `RequestBooking` use case. An advisor asks one service to take the patient on an open case. It stores a booking Task in `requested` state in Neo4j, writes the audit record and the outbound request message in the same transaction, and schedules the 5-minute phone prompt and the 10-minute expiry. It sends nothing itself.

**Rules you need.**
- One open (`requested`) booking per service; a second request raises `Conflict` until the first is answered, expires or is cancelled.
- `reply_by = requested_at + 10 min`; advisor phone prompt at +5 min; expiry at +10 min. Silence is never acceptance.
- The case must be open with age band, needs and urgency; otherwise `ValidationFailed`.
- Reference: 4 random digits unique among open bookings, retry on collision [ASSUMED].
- Channel: text → B1, email → B2, push → B3. Fields: reference, service name, age band, need (first need, or two joined with "and"), urgency, link, reply_by "HH:MM" London. Never the postcode.
- Only `advisor` and `clinical`.

**Build.** `application/use_cases/request_booking.py`: `RequestBookingCommand(idempotency_key, actor, case_id, service_id)`; `RequestBookingResult(booking_id, reference, reply_by, phone_prompt_at)`; `RequestBooking(uow_factory, idempotency, link_tokens, clock, ids, rng: random.Random, query_board)`. In one unit of work: save the booking (`requested`); audit `AuditEntry(action="C", use_case="RequestBooking", ...)` with snapshot `{service_id, status, effective_available, data_age_minutes, pending}`; outbox `booking.events` `BookingRequested` (session id = service id) and the rendered request on `messages.outbound` with a `Communication`; `schedule_work` for `BOOKING_PHONE_PROMPT` (+5) and `BOOKING_EXPIRE` (+10) about the booking id; a booking link token (purpose `booking`, expires at reply-by) for text and email.
