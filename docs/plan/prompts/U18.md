**Goal.** Build `RespondToBooking`: the service confirms or declines an open booking request from any channel (text, link, app). It moves the booking Task in Neo4j, cancels the pending timers, queues the echo and tells the advisor through an event.

**Read first.**
- `application/use_cases/request_booking.py` (U17) and its tests (seeding).
- `domain/rules/booking_state.py` (D13): `transition`, `to_fhir`.
- `domain/messages/templates.py`: B4, B5.
- `graph_store/scheduled_work_repository.py` (P08) `for_about`; `application/scheduling.py` (U03) `cancel_work`.
- `domain/model/booking.py`, `graph_store/booking_repository.py` (P02): `Booking.with_status(target, at, reason)` (sets `confirmed_at`, `responded_at`, `reason`), `get`, `save`, `confirmed_since`.
- `application/idempotency.py` (P15); `docs/spec/domain-rules.md` 1.6 and 3.

**Rules you need.**
- Only from `requested`. Confirm → `confirmed` (Task.status `accepted`, businessStatus `confirmed`). Decline → `declined` (`rejected`/`declined`). Other states raise `InvalidTransition`; a reply to an expired booking returns `already_final=True` and changes nothing.
- Optional decline reason: `full`, `outside-criteria`, `staffing-or-transport`, `other`. It can arrive later: `SetDeclineReason` on a declined booking without a reason.
- A confirmation reduces effective available by 1 (`responded_at` counts in `confirmed_since`) and clears pending; a decline clears pending.
- Actor: reporter for the service (`actor.service_ids`) or `anonymous-link` scoped to the service.
- Cancel both timers with `cancel_work`.

**Build.** `application/use_cases/respond_to_booking.py`: `RespondToBookingCommand(idempotency_key, actor, booking_id, decision: Literal["confirm", "decline"], reason: DeclineReason | None = None, channel: ReportSource)`; `RespondToBookingResult(booking_id, status, responded_at, already_final: bool)`; `RespondToBooking(uow_factory, idempotency, clock, ids)`; `SetDeclineReasonCommand(idempotency_key, actor, booking_id, reason)` and `SetDeclineReason`. Outbox `booking.events` `BookingResponded` (session id = service id) plus B4/B5 on `messages.outbound` with a `Communication`. Audit `use_case="RespondToBooking"`.

1. Confirm at 14:19 → read back `confirmed`, `responded_at` 14:19; Task node `status="accepted"`, `businessStatus="confirmed"`; both `ScheduledWork` nodes `cancelled` with two `CancelWork` outbox nodes; `BookingResponded` and a B4 text "Booked: <reference>, UCR West, confirmed 14:19. Thank you."
