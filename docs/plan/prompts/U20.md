**Goal.** Build `CancelBooking`: the advisor cancels a booking before or after the service confirmed it. The service is told by its own channel and the slot goes back.

**Read first.**
- `application/use_cases/request_booking.py` (U17), `respond_to_booking.py` (U18).
- `domain/rules/booking_state.py` (D13); `templates.py` B6; `application/scheduling.py` (U03) `cancel_work`.
- `graph_store/booking_repository.py` (P02): `confirmed_since`.
- `docs/spec/ui-screens.md` "BookingExceptions" screens 6 and 7.

**Rules you need.**
- `requested` → `cancelled-before`; `confirmed` → `cancelled-after`; Task.status `cancelled`. Other states → `InvalidTransition`.
- Required reason: `caller-declined`, `no-longer-needed`, `other-route-found`, `entered-in-error`.
- Cancelling `requested` cancels both timers and releases pending. Cancelling `confirmed` means the slot no longer reduces effective available; P02's `confirmed_since` already counts only bookings whose status is exactly `confirmed`, so prove it end to end with `QueryBoard`.
- Notify the service with B6 ("Booking 4821 was cancelled at 14:40. No action needed.").
- Only `advisor` and `clinical`.

**Build.** `application/use_cases/cancel_booking.py`: `CancelBookingCommand(idempotency_key, actor, booking_id, reason: CancelReason)`, `CancelBookingResult(booking_id, status)`, `CancelBooking(uow_factory, idempotency, clock, ids)`. Append `BookingCancelled(booking_id, service_id, status, at)` to `domain/events.py`. Outbox `booking.events` `BookingCancelled` (session id = service id) plus B6. Audit `use_case="CancelBooking"`.

**Tests to write first.** `tests/integration/application/test_cancel_booking.py`, real Neo4j.
1. Cancel `requested` at 14:20 with `caller-declined` → read back `cancelled-before`; timers `cancelled`; `BookingCancelled` and B6 outbox nodes.
2. Cancel `confirmed` → `cancelled-after`; `QueryBoard` shows the reported figure again.
3. Cancel `declined` → `InvalidTransition`; nothing written.
4. Missing reason rejected at command validation; reporter → `NotAllowed`.
5. Replay → one audit record.
