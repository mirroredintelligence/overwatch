**The product.** DIUCS capacity helps an NHS urgent-care single point of access (SPOA) in Dorset. Community services, wards and virtual wards report how many referrals or beds they can take, by text message, a one-tap link, or an installed web app. Call advisors see a live wall board and a filtered list of services that fit a patient's needs, then ask a service to take the patient by text and wait for CONFIRMED. Shift leads chase late reports, register services and keep criteria current. Nothing anywhere holds a patient name or NHS number.

**The architecture.** Hexagonal. A Python back end (package `diucs` in `backend/src/diucs`) with four layers: `domain` (pure rules, no I/O), `application` (use cases and ports defined as `typing.Protocol`), `adapters` (inbound: FHIR REST, SMS webhook, link pages, workers; outbound: Neo4j, Redis, Azure Service Bus, SMS, email, push; `adapters/testing`: fakes for external providers only) and `bootstrap` (settings and the composition root). Dependencies point inwards only. A Next.js front end in `frontend/` talks only to its own `/api/bff/*` route handlers, which call the back end's FHIR R4B API. Neo4j is the only database, Redis is a cache, Azure Service Bus carries messages and scheduled work.

**Goal.** Build registration drafting and submission: the applicant fills five steps (organisation, location, service, capacity reporting, API), saves as they go, and submits for approval. Drafts persist in Neo4j between sessions; validation returns field errors for the form's error summary.

**Read first.**
- `domain/rules/registration.py` (D22): `transition_registration`.
- `domain/model/registration.py`, `RegistrationRepository` and `Neo4jRegistrationRepository` (P13).
- `backend/tests/support/uow.py` (P06), `application/idempotency.py` (P15), harness helpers (F10).
- `docs/spec/ui-screens.md` artboards "RegisterFlow1", "RegisterFlow2", "RegisterFlow3", "ApprovalApplicant" screen 1 and "RegisterModel" (required fields).

**Rules you need.**
- Service name and ODS code come from the invitation and cannot be changed.
- Required per step. Organisation: name, ODS code, type (NHS trust, GP practice, local authority, voluntary or independent, other), contact name, contact email or phone. Location: fixed site yes/no; if yes, postcode, chosen address, site name, site type (ward, community hospital, hub or base, clinic, other), opening days and times or 24 hours. Service: category, at least one referral method (phone, SystmOne, email, e-Referral), SPOA phone (a full UK number), accepting days and times, at least one accepted need, youngest age, oldest age or "no limit", excluded needs or "no exclusions", criteria owner role, owner email, review-by date, postcode districts (required when there is no fixed site). Reporting: measure, lowest and highest usual number (low <= high), cadence, channel, mobile for text or push or email for email, who holds the phone. API: optional; if "yes", name, https address, connection type, data type.
- Error messages use the screen wording: "Choose a category", "Enter a phone number the SPOA can call", "Enter a full phone number, for example 01305 123456", "Enter who owns the criteria".
- First save moves `invited` → `draft`. Submit only from `draft` or `needs-changes`, only when every step is complete; it moves to `submitted` and records the time. Drafts never appear on the board.
- The reporting mobile or email is written to the `ContactStore` (P16), never into the draft node.

**Build.** `backend/src/diucs/application/use_cases/registration_draft.py`:
- Step dataclasses `OrganisationStep`, `LocationStep`, `ServiceStep`, `ReportingStep`, `ApiStep`; `RegistrationDraft(registration_id, organisation, location, service, reporting, api)` with each step optional. Persist the draft in P13's `Registration.draft` dict (one key per step) through `Neo4jRegistrationRepository`.
- `FieldError(field: str, message: str)`; `validate_step(step) -> list[FieldError]` (pure; unit-test it in `tests/unit`).
- `SaveRegistrationStepCommand(idempotency_key, actor, registration_id, step)` → `SaveStepResult(errors: tuple[FieldError, ...], status)`. Saves even with errors and reports them.
- `SubmitRegistrationCommand(idempotency_key, actor, registration_id)` → `SubmitResult(status, errors)`; emits `registration.events/RegistrationSubmitted`.
- The actor must be the invited applicant (the practitioner id on the registration).

**Tests to write first.** Pure validation cases in `tests/unit/application/test_registration_validation.py`; the rest in `tests/integration/application/test_registration_draft.py`:
1. Save the organisation step on an `invited` registration; in a fresh unit of work the status is `draft` and the step reads back equal (`assert_round_trip`).
2. Service step with no category and phone "0123": two field errors with the exact messages above.
3. Changing the service name raises `ValidationFailed("service_name", ...)`.
4. Visiting service (no fixed site) without districts: error on districts.
5. Submit with the reporting step missing: errors returned, stored status still `draft`.
6. Complete submit: stored status `submitted`, `RegistrationSubmitted` outbox node, one audit node.
7. Resubmit from `needs-changes` works; submit from `in-review` raises `InvalidTransition` and changes nothing.
8. The reporting mobile appears only on the protected contact node.
