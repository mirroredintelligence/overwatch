**The product.** DIUCS capacity helps an NHS urgent-care single point of access (SPOA) in Dorset. Community services, wards and virtual wards report how many referrals or beds they can take, by text message, a one-tap link, or an installed web app. Call advisors see a live wall board and a filtered list of services that fit a patient's needs, then ask a service to take the patient by text and wait for CONFIRMED. Dependencies point inwards only. A Next.js front end in `frontend/` talks only to its own `/api/bff/*` route handlers, which call the back end's FHIR R4B API. **Goal.** On submit, turn a complete registration draft into draft FHIR R4B resources (Organization, Location, HealthcareService and the first ServiceCriteria as Basic) and write them to Neo4j in one transaction, with the geocoded position and the ODS check result.

**Read first.**
- `backend/src/diucs/application/use_cases/registration_draft.py` (U26).
- `adapters/outbound/graph_store/resource_store.py` (G05), `mapper.py` (G02-G04) and `extensions.py` for `ext_*` names.
- `RegistrationRepository` / `Neo4jRegistrationRepository` (P13); `backend/tests/support/uow.py` (P06).
- `application/ports/outbound/geo.py` and `terminology.py` (P11): use `FixedGeocoder` and `FixedTerminology` in tests (external providers).
- `docs/spec/fhir-data-model.md` sections "Services and places" and "Criteria"; artboard "RegisterModel" in `docs/spec/ui-screens.md`; `docs/spec/architecture.md` flow "F4. Registration and approval".

**Rules you need.**
- Everything is a draft: `Organization.active=false`, `Location.status="inactive"`, `HealthcareService.active=false`, criteria version 1 with status `pending-approval`.
- One transaction: the four resources, the audit entry and the outbox message commit together or not at all.
- Location position comes from the chosen address candidate. Keep the UPRN as an identifier and set `ext_geocodeSource="os-places"` and `ext_geocodedAt`. Visiting services have no position; their districts become coverage-area Locations with `mode="kind"` and type postcode-district (reuse an existing district Location when present).
- Service extensions: `ext_establishedCapacity` (the highest usual number unless given), `ext_usualRangeMin`, `ext_usualRangeMax`, `ext_reportCadence`, `ext_limitedLevelPercent` (80), `ext_smsShortName` (service name).
- The reporter's phone or email is not in any FHIR resource; it stays in the `ContactStore`.
- Store the ODS lookup result (found, active, name) on the registration for U28. Do not fail the submit when ODS is not found.

**Build.**
- `backend/src/diucs/application/use_cases/registration_submit.py`: pure `build_registration_bundle(draft, ids, now, address: AddressCandidate | None) -> dict` returning a FHIR `Bundle` of type `transaction`; `PersistRegistration(uow_factory, geocoder, terminology, ids, clock)`, called by `SubmitRegistration` after a successful submit.
- Add `write_bundle(bundle)` to `Neo4jRegistrationRepository` (P13's file), writing each entry through the G05 resource store inside the current unit-of-work transaction and resolving `urn:uuid:` references to the new ids.

**Tests to write first.** Bundle-building cases in `tests/unit`; persistence in `tests/integration/application/test_registration_submit.py`:
1. Fixed-site draft → bundle with 4 entries, each `request.method="POST"`, cross-references as `urn:uuid:` full URLs; each resource validates with the `fhir.resources` R4B models.
2. Visiting service → no position; two coverage-area Locations for DT3 and DT4; a second registration covering DT3 reuses the existing DT3 Location (`count_nodes` stays 2).
3. No phone number or email appears anywhere in the bundle JSON.
4. Persist, then load the HealthcareService through the resource store in a fresh session: its `json` equals the bundle entry's resource after id resolution, and the `PROVIDED_BY`, `LOCATION` and `CURRENT_CRITERIA` relationships exist.
5. Force a failure on the last write (a repository stub that raises): nothing from the bundle, no audit node and no outbox node remains.
