**Goal.** Build `SubscribeAlert`: a COO, ICB lead or shift lead chooses which alerts they get, the not-updated threshold, quiet hours, how fast they must acknowledge, and their deputy. Preferences persist in Neo4j through the P17 repository.

**Rules you need.**
- Kinds: `service-closed` (not planned out-of-hours closures), `over-capacity`, `not-updated` (threshold 2 or 4 hours), `system-pressure` (5 or more services limited or worse). Plus daily summary (07:30 by notification) and weekly summary (Mondays by email).
- Defaults for a new user: all four kinds on, not-updated 2 hours, daily summary on, weekly summary off, no quiet hours, acknowledge within 15 minutes, everything during quiet hours.
- Quiet hours: none, or 22:00 to 06:00. During quiet hours: critical only (`service-closed`, `system-pressure` [ASSUMED]) or everything.
- Acknowledge window: 10, 15 or 30 minutes. The deputy must be another practitioner with role `exec` or `lead`; required when any kind is on.
- One Subscription per user per kind (status `active` or `off`) pointing at the `SubscriptionTopic` for that kind; filter values as properties. The R4B element names are not verified, so the stored shape stays internal and R12 maps it.
- Roles `exec` and `lead` only; a user changes only their own preferences.

**Build.** `backend/src/diucs/application/use_cases/subscribe_alert.py`: `SaveAlertPreferencesCommand(idempotency_key, actor, kinds, not_updated_hours, quiet_hours, quiet_critical_only, ack_minutes, deputy_id, daily_summary, weekly_summary)`; `SaveAlertPreferences(uow_factory, idempotency, clock)`; `GetAlertPreferences(uow_factory)` returning defaults when nothing is saved. Store and return P17's `AlertSubscription`.

**Tests to write first.** In `tests/integration/application/test_subscribe_alert.py`, seeding an exec, a deputy exec and a reporter:
1. Get for a new exec user returns the defaults above; nothing is written.
2. Save with `not_updated_hours=3` or `ack_minutes=20` raises `ValidationFailed`; no Subscription nodes.
3. Deputy equal to self, or the reporter, raises `ValidationFailed`.
4. Save, then in a fresh unit of work `subscribers_for_kind(over-capacity)` returns the user and four Subscription nodes exist; turning over-capacity off removes them from that query.
5. Preferences round-trip: saved quiet hours 22:00-06:00 and deputy read back equal.
6. Advisor role raises `NotAllowed`; one exec cannot change another's preferences.
7. The audit node records the kinds chosen and nothing else personal.
