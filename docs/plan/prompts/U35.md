**Goal.** Build the week summary query for COO and ICB users: referrals, how often the best fit was not available, on-time capacity updates, median time to a confirmed booking, and updates by channel, with groups under 10 hidden. All figures come from Cypher over the real graph.

- The Neo4j repositories: `Neo4jCaseRepository`, `Neo4jBookingRepository` (P02), `Neo4jCapacityRepository` (P01), `Neo4jCommunicationRepository` (P07), and the named-query registry (G01).

**Rules you need.**
- Week = Monday 00:00 to Sunday 24:00 London, converted to UTC.
- Referrals = cases opened in the week.
- Best fit not available = cases closed with outcome `no-service-fits`, or with `best_fit_not_used` in (`full`, `declined`, `data-too-old`) [ASSUMED definition], as a share of referrals.
- Capacity updates on time = reports received at or before reply-by for prompts due in the week, divided by prompts due.
- Median time to a confirmed booking = minutes from request to confirmation, over bookings confirmed in the week.
- Updates by channel = reports by source `text`, `app`, `email`/`link`.
- Time back is an estimate: "N updates by text, app or email. Each replaces about one phone call of [x] minutes." `x` comes from `Settings.minutes_per_avoided_call`, which may be unset; then show "[x]". Always label it "Estimate".
- Any count under 10 is returned hidden (None plus `hidden=True`); a percentage or median whose base is under 10 is hidden too. Outcomes after referral are not shown.

**Build.** Add `opened_between(start, end)` to `CaseRepository`, `reports_between` to `CapacityRepository`, `confirmed_between` to `BookingRepository`, and `prompts_due_between` to `CommunicationRepository`: each to the Protocol and the Neo4j adapter, with named queries (`Q-CASE-OPENED-BETWEEN`, ...) and a round-trip test each. `backend/src/diucs/application/use_cases/week_summary.py`: `Measure(value: float | int | None, hidden: bool, label: str)`; `WeekSummary(week_start: date, referrals, best_fit_unavailable_pct, on_time_pct, median_minutes_to_confirmed, updates_by_channel: dict[str, Measure], time_back_text: str)`; `GetWeekSummary(uow_factory, settings, clock)` with `execute(GetWeekSummaryCommand(actor, week_start))`. Roles `exec` and `lead`.
