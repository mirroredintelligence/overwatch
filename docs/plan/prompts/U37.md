**Goal.** Build the people-access use cases: the lead removes a person's access, a person signs out of every phone, and a reporter who lost a phone moves to a new one after a lead call-back. Roles, devices and credentials are changed in Neo4j and checked there.

**Rules you need.**
- Remove person (lead only): reasons left the service, changed role, lost phone, other. Effects: every active PractitionerRole for that person ends (`active=False`, `period_end=now`); every device signed out and its credential deleted; push subscriptions removed; past reports stay. For each service that now has no active reporter, prompts go to the cover number if a `COVERED_BY` cover is active, otherwise to the shift lead: record a service flag `no-reporter` [ASSUMED code] for the lead page.
- Sign out everywhere (the person themself): all their devices signed out; they sign in again with a text code and PIN. The result says how many phones were signed out.
- Lost phone: the reporter verifies a text code on the new phone (purpose `new-device`); all old devices are signed out at once; the new device is registered as `pending-callback` and cannot receive prompts or sign in until the lead records a call-back on the register number (`ConfirmNewPhone`). Until then prompts go to the cover number or the SPOA.
- Every action is audited; no phone numbers in audit entries.

**Build.** `backend/src/diucs/application/use_cases/remove_person.py`: `RemovePerson`, `SignOutEverywhere`, `StartNewPhone`, `ConfirmNewPhone`, each with command and result. If P16's port lacks `revoke_all(practitioner_id)` or the device status `pending-callback`, add them to the Protocol and the Neo4j adapter together, with round-trip tests.
