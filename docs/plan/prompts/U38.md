**Rules you need.**
- Move (lead): service, from phone, to mobile, and "until I change it" or "cover until a date". Three steps in order: (1) text a code to the new phone and it is verified; (2) the lead phones the service on the register number and records it; (3) the service's reporter role moves to the new phone. The old phone keeps its other services. A cover sets `COVERED_BY {until}` instead of ending the old role, and ends automatically at `until`.
- Add a service to a phone (lead): the person sees "UCR North has been added. Do you look after it?" and accepts or rejects. Only on acceptance is the role active; the usual registration checks still apply (no live prompts until the service is live).
- After any change, if the phone holds more than 3 services, set flag `holds-many-services` for the lead's phones list.
- The role carries the service link, so a phone with three services is one Practitioner with three PractitionerRoles.

**Build.** `backend/src/diucs/application/use_cases/move_service.py`: `StartServiceMove`, `RecordMoveCallBack`, `CompleteServiceMove`, `AddServiceToPhone`, `AnswerServiceAddition`, with commands and results, using P20's `ServiceMove` record (set `cancelled_at` if a move is abandoned).

**Tests to write first.** In `tests/integration/application/test_move_service.py`, seeding one phone with UCR West and UCR East:
1. Complete a permanent move: in a fresh unit of work the old UCR East role is ended, a new active role links UCR East to the new phone's practitioner, UCR West unchanged on the old phone; the move record reads back with all three timestamps.
2. `CompleteServiceMove` before the call-back raises `NotAllowed` naming the missing step; roles unchanged.
3. Cover until Friday 20:00: old role kept; a `COVERED_BY {until}` relationship exists; after `until` the services for the old phone hash include UCR East again.
4. Add UCR North: role stored inactive until the person accepts; rejection ends it.
5. A fourth service on one phone sets the `holds-many-services` flag.
6. Reporter role cannot start a move.
