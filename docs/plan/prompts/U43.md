**Goal.** Build the planning analysis: from the decision snapshots and case outcomes, show where capacity ran out and where patients went instead. Counts under 10 are always hidden.

**Rules you need.**
- Cohort filters: period last 30, 90 or 365 days; need group (all, falls and frailty, IV therapy, palliative); default cohort age 65+ (bands 65-79 and 80+), urgency within 2 hrs, referrers ambulance crew or care home.
- Best fit = the first service in the options snapshot (the list is sorted by travel time) [ASSUMED]. Best fit unavailable = its status was `not-accepting`, `over-capacity` or `not-updated`, or its effective available was 0.
- Outputs: referrals; best fit unavailable (count and share); went to another suitable service (destination was in the snapshot's passing list); table of best fit and its state → destination → count; heat grid of best-fit-unavailable by weekday × 6-hour band (00-06, 06-12, 12-18, 18-24, London time) with the busiest cell; declined despite showing capacity by service.
- Every count under 10 is returned as hidden ("fewer than 10"); a share whose base is under 10 is hidden.
- Reads only (no writes except an audit entry for the query). Roles `lead` and `exec` only [ASSUMED]. No patient outcomes.
- Do the grouping in Cypher where it is simple, and in Python over a bounded result otherwise; name every query (`Q-PLAN-*`).
