**Goal.** Keep the wall board current: listen to the server-sent event stream, refetch the board when something changes, poll every minute as a fallback, and switch to a clear "Last known" view when both fail.

**Rules you need.**
- The back end streams `GET /events` (text/event-stream) with events such as `event: board.updated` `data: {"serviceId":"a1"}` and `event: booking.updated`. Comment lines (`: ping`) keep it alive.
- `GET /api/bff/events` relays the back-end stream for a signed-in session, passing the session's back-end token. It streams through without buffering, forwards only `board.updated` and `booking.updated` events, and closes the upstream when the browser disconnects.
- `LiveBoard` (client) holds the last good `Board`. On `board.updated` it refetches `/api/bff/board`. It also refetches every 60 seconds.
- Stream state: if the EventSource is down for more than 10 seconds, mark the stream as gapped.
- Offline: when the stream is gapped and the last refetch failed, show the offline view until a refetch succeeds:
  - Banner (role alert): "Tool offline since 14:02" (time of the first failure) and "Figures are the last known and may be wrong. Use the whiteboard and phone process."
  - Every tile switches to the not-updated look with badge "Last known", meta "Last known 13:58" (the tile's `reportedAt`), and age line "Do not rely on this. Phone to confirm." The number stays as last known.
- Recovery: one successful refetch clears the banner and restores normal tiles.
- Announce nothing on every refresh (no live-region noise); the offline banner is the only announcement.
