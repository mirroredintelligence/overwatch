**The product.** DIUCS capacity helps an NHS urgent-care single point of access (SPOA) in Dorset. Community services, wards and virtual wards report how many referrals or beds they can take, by text message, a one-tap link, or an installed web app. Call advisors see a live wall board and a filtered list of services that fit a patient's needs, then ask a service to take the patient by text and wait for CONFIRMED. Neo4j is the only database, Redis is a cache, Azure Service Bus carries messages and scheduled work.

**Goal.** Let staff (advisors, clinical advisors, shift leads, criteria owners) sign in with their Trust Microsoft account, turn that into a BFF session with a back-end token, and guard each route group by role.

**Read first.**
- `frontend/lib/auth/session.ts`, `frontend/lib/bff/handler.ts` (W02).
- `backend/src/diucs/adapters/inbound/fhir_rest/auth.py` (R02): the JWT claims and signing the back end accepts. Match it exactly; if it differs from below, follow R02 and note it.
- `docs/spec/ui-screens.md`, artboards "AdvisorStart" (Sign in) and "ClinicalAdvisor" (step 1); `docs/spec/architecture.md` "Security, IG and operations" → Staff sign-in.

**Rules you need.**
- OpenID Connect against Microsoft Entra ID with Auth.js (`next-auth` v5) and its `MicrosoftEntraID` provider; env `ENTRA_TENANT_ID`, `ENTRA_CLIENT_ID`, `ENTRA_CLIENT_SECRET`. Use Auth.js only for the handshake. On successful sign-in create our own session (W02) and do not keep Auth.js's session cookie.
- Role mapping from the Entra `roles` claim (app roles): `DIUCS.Advisor` → advisor, `DIUCS.Clinical` → clinical, `DIUCS.Lead` → lead, `DIUCS.Owner` → owner, `DIUCS.Planning` → exec, `DIUCS.Support` → support. Several roles: pick by the priority lead, clinical, advisor, owner, exec, support. No mapped role → no session; show "Your account does not have access to DIUCS capacity. Ask your shift lead to check your access."
- Back-end token: HS256 JWT signed with `BACKEND_JWT_SECRET`; `sub` = the Entra object id (`oid`), `role`, `services` (staff roles are not service-scoped: use whatever value R02 defines for "all services"), `exp` 8 hours. Never put the user's name or email in claims or logs.
- Sign-in page `/sign-in`: "DIUCS capacity", "Sign in", "Use your Trust staff account. There is no new password.", button "Sign in with your Trust account", "Having trouble? Ask your shift lead to check your access." With `?role=clinical` also show "Clinical advisors see case details, so this uses staff sign-in, not a texted code."
- `middleware.ts` guards by path prefix: `/advisor` and `/advisor/case` → advisor or lead; `/clinical` → clinical; `/lead` → lead; `/owner` → owner or clinical; `/planning` → exec or lead; `/console` → support. No session → redirect to `/sign-in?next=<path>`. Wrong role → rewrite to `/forbidden`, which shows "You do not have access to this page."
- After sign-in, go to `next` if it is a same-origin path, otherwise the role's home (`/advisor`, `/clinical`, `/lead`, `/owner/criteria`).

**Build.** `lib/auth/staff.ts` (`mapRoles`, `mintBackendToken`, `safeNext`), the Auth.js config and a callback that calls `createSession`, `app/sign-in/page.tsx` (URL `/sign-in`), `app/forbidden/page.tsx`, `middleware.ts`.

**Tests to write first.**

6. Middleware: advisor on `/advisor/case/4821` → allowed.
7. Sign-in page renders the copy, and the clinical note only with `?role=clinical`.
8. Callback with no mapped role → no session cookie, access message shown.
9. `vitest-axe` clean on the sign-in and forbidden pages.
