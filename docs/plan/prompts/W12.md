**The product.** DIUCS capacity helps an NHS urgent-care single point of access (SPOA) in Dorset. Community services, wards and virtual wards report how many referrals or beds they can take, by text message, a one-tap link, or an installed web app. Call advisors see a live wall board and a filtered list of services that fit a patient's needs, then ask a service to take the patient by text and wait for CONFIRMED. Shift leads chase late reports, register services and keep criteria current. Nothing anywhere holds a patient name or NHS number.

**The architecture.** Hexagonal. A Python back end (package `diucs` in `backend/src/diucs`) with four layers: `domain` (pure rules, no I/O), `application` (use cases and ports defined as `typing.Protocol`), `adapters` (inbound: FHIR REST, SMS webhook, link pages, workers; outbound: Neo4j, Redis, Azure Service Bus, SMS, email, push; `adapters/testing`: fakes for external providers only) and `bootstrap` (settings and the composition root). Dependencies point inwards only. A Next.js front end in `frontend/` talks only to its own `/api/bff/*` route handlers, which call the back end's FHIR R4B API. Neo4j is the only database, Redis is a cache, Azure Service Bus carries messages and scheduled work.

**Rules you need.**

- NHS Pathways Dx: sub "Used by 111 and ambulance telephone triage"; search field "Search Dx code or description" calling `GET /api/bff/terminology/pathways-dx?q=<text>` (at least 2 characters, debounced 250 ms) → `[{ code, display }]`; results as option buttons "<code> · <display>"; note "Choose from the list. - Ambulance category: sub "Used by 999"; options "Category 1" Life-threatening, "Category 2" Emergency, "Category 3" Urgent, "Category 4" Less urgent; note "Recorded as the crew gave it."
- Manchester triage: sub "Used by emergency departments"; options Immediate, Very urgent, Urgent, Standard, Non-urgent; note "Only for referrers who have already triaged in an emergency department."
- NEWS2: field "NEWS2 score (0 to 20)", whole numbers only; error "Enter a whole number from 0 to 20".
- Clinical Frailty Scale: "Clinical Frailty Scale (1 to 9)", chips 1 to 9; under them the chosen value and label: 1 Very fit, 2 Well, 3 Managing well, 4 Vulnerable, 5 Mildly frail, 6 Moderately frail, 7 Severely frail, 8 Very severely frail, 9 Terminally ill ("6 · Moderately frail").
