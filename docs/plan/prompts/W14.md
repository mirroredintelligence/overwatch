**The product.** DIUCS capacity helps an NHS urgent-care single point of access (SPOA) in Dorset. Community services, wards and virtual wards report how many referrals or beds they can take, by text message, a one-tap link, or an installed web app. Call advisors see a live wall board and a filtered list of services that fit a patient's needs, then ask a service to take the patient by text and wait for CONFIRMED. Shift leads chase late reports, register services and keep criteria current. Nothing anywhere holds a patient name or NHS number.

**The architecture.** Hexagonal. A Python back end (package `diucs` in `backend/src/diucs`) with four layers: `domain` (pure rules, no I/O), `application` (use cases and ports defined as `typing.Protocol`), `adapters` (inbound: FHIR REST, SMS webhook, link pages, workers; outbound: Neo4j, Redis, Azure Service Bus, SMS, email, push; `adapters/testing`: fakes for external providers only) and `bootstrap` (settings and the composition root). Dependencies point inwards only. A Next.js front end in `frontend/` talks only to its own `/api/bff/*` route handlers, which call the back end's FHIR R4B API. Neo4j is the only database, Redis is a cache, Azure Service Bus carries messages and scheduled work.

**Rules you need.**

- Criteria dialog: heading "Criteria: UCR West", sub "Full view"; "Accepts" then "Falls without injury · Frailty · Age 18+ · Postcodes DT1 to DT4 · 08:00 to 20:00"; "Does not accept" then "Suspected fracture · Head injury on anticoagulants · Needs IV therapy"; meta "Version 3 · reviewed 12 Sep · review by 12 Dec · owner: UCR clinical lead". When `overdue`, a warning "Criteria not reviewed since 12 Sep". Buttons "Report a problem with these criteria" (link `/help?topic=criteria&service=<id>`) and "Close". Data: `GET /api/bff/services/[id]/criteria` → `{ accepts: string, excludes: string, version, reviewedOn, reviewBy, owner, overdue }`.
- I phoned: heading "I phoned", sub the service name; "What did UCR West tell you?" options Accepting, Limited, Not accepting; "How many can they take?" number field (hidden, and sent as 0, when Not accepting); buttons "Save position" and "Cancel"; note "Updates the board for everyone. Success closes the dialog and calls `onSaved` so the options can refresh. Error "Enter how many they can take, as a whole number".
- They declined: heading "They declined", sub "The board showed capacity"; "Why did UCR West decline?" options "Full, not yet reported", "Outside their criteria", "Staffing or transport", "Other"; buttons "Save" and "Cancel"; note "For a conversation with the service. Not used to assess advisors." Posts `POST /api/bff/services/[id]/declined` `{ caseRef, reason: "full" | "outside-criteria" | "staffing-or-transport" | "other" }`.
