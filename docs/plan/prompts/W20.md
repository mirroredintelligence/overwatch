**The product.** DIUCS capacity helps an NHS urgent-care single point of access (SPOA) in Dorset. Community services, wards and virtual wards report how many referrals or beds they can take, by text message, a one-tap link, or an installed web app. Call advisors see a live wall board and a filtered list of services that fit a patient's needs, then ask a service to take the patient by text and wait for CONFIRMED. Shift leads chase late reports, register services and keep criteria current. Nothing anywhere holds a patient name or NHS number.

**The architecture.** Hexagonal. A Python back end (package `diucs` in `backend/src/diucs`) with four layers: `domain` (pure rules, no I/O), `application` (use cases and ports defined as `typing.Protocol`), `adapters` (inbound: FHIR REST, SMS webhook, link pages, workers; outbound: Neo4j, Redis, Azure Service Bus, SMS, email, push; `adapters/testing`: fakes for external providers only) and `bootstrap` (settings and the composition root). Dependencies point inwards only. A Next.js front end in `frontend/` talks only to its own `/api/bff/*` route handlers, which call the back end's FHIR R4B API. Neo4j is the only database, Redis is a cache, Azure Service Bus carries messages and scheduled work.

**Rules you need.**
- BFF `POST /api/bff/bookings` `{ caseRef, serviceId }` (advisor, CSRF, `Idempotency-Key`) → back-end `POST /fhir/Task` → `{ id, reference, status: "requested", requestedAt, replyBy, phonePromptAt, delivery: "sent" | "delivered" | "failed" }`. 409 from the back end (one open request per service) → `{ code: "open-request" }`. `GET /api/bff/bookings/[id]` → the same shape plus `confirmedAt`, `declinedAt`, `declineReason`, `expiredAt`.
- The card listens on the existing `/api/bff/events` stream for `booking.updated` with its `bookingId` and refetches. It also refetches every 30 seconds.
- Requested: badge "Requested", "Not booked yet", "Text sent 14:16 · delivered. Waiting for CONFIRMED. Phone prompt at 14:21. Request expires 14:26.", "Colleagues see 1 pending on the board.", buttons "Phone them instead" (shows the service phone as text with a `tel:` link) and "Cancel request" (calls `onCancel`, wired in W21). `CallSteps current="request"` with "Now: waiting for UCR West to confirm. Phone prompt at 14:21."
- At `phonePromptAt` with status still requested: an alert (`role="alert"`) "No reply yet. Phone UCR West now." (client timer, not waiting for the server).
- Booked: badge "Booked", "Confirmed 14:19", "Reference 4821. Capacity reduced by 1. Next: complete the referral in SystmOne.", buttons "Copy summary for SystmOne" (link to the finish summary) and "Record outcome".
- Declined: "Declined", "Not booked", "Declined 14:19. Reason: outside criteria. Pending count released. Choose another option or phone to discuss.", buttons "Back to options" and "Phone to discuss". No reason: "Reason: not given."
- Expired: "No reply", "Not booked. Phone now.", "No reply after 5 min, so you were prompted to phone. The request expired at 10 min and the pending count is released. Do not assume it was accepted.", buttons "Phone UCR West" and "Try next option".
- The "Request booking" button is busy while posting and cannot double-post. A 409 shows "UCR West already has a request waiting. Phone them instead."
- Status changes are announced once in a polite live region ("UCR West confirmed booking 4821").
- data-el: `ADV-OPT-REQUEST-BTN` (existing), `ADV-BOOK-PHONE-BTN`, `ADV-BOOK-CANCEL-BTN`, `ADV-BOOK-OUTCOME-BTN`, `ADV-BOOK-NEXT-BTN`.
