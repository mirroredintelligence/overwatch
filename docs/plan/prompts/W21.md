**Goal.** Let the advisor cancel a request or a booking with a reason, and alert the advisor at once when a service withdraws a confirmed booking or cannot reach the patient.

**Read first.**

- `docs/spec/ui-screens.md`, artboard "BookingExceptions" panels 5 and 6; `docs/spec/domain-rules.md` section 3.2.

**Rules you need.**
- BFF `POST /api/bff/bookings/[id]/cancel` `{ reason: "caller-declined" | "no-longer-needed" | "other-route-found" | "entered-in-error" }` (advisor, CSRF, `Idempotency-Key`) → back-end `POST /fhir/Task/{id}/$cancel` → `{ status: "cancelled-before" | "cancelled-after" }`. 409 when the booking moved on (for example just confirmed) → `{ code: "state-changed", status }`.
- Cancel dialog for a confirmed booking: heading "Cancel booking", question "Cancel the booking with UCR West?", reasons "Caller declined", "No longer needed", "Other route found", "Entered in error"; note "The service is told by its own channel. The slot goes back to available."; buttons "Cancel booking" (needs a reason) and "Keep booking". For a request not yet answered, the question is "Cancel the request to UCR West?" and the buttons "Cancel request" and "Keep request" [ASSUMED wording, same reasons].
- After cancelling, the card shows "Cancelled" and "Choose another option" returns to the list. On 409, refetch and show "This booking changed before you cancelled. Check its status."
- Withdrawal alert, shown as an alert dialog (`role="alertdialog"`, focus moves to it) when the booking turns `withdrawn`: title "UCR West withdrew", badge "Withdrawn", heading "UCR West can no longer take this patient", "Reason: staffing. Withdrawn 14:41.", "Phone the caller now.", "Tell them another service is being found.", "If nobody acts within 10 minutes the shift lead is alerted.", buttons "See options again", "Ask a clinical advisor" (prop `onAskClinical`), "Phone the caller".
- Not-reached alert, same pattern: title "UCR West could not reach the patient", reason "Not at the address" (or "No answer at the door", "Gone to hospital", "Other"), "The SPOA is told and will phone the caller.", buttons "See options again" and "Phone the caller" [ASSUMED wording].
- "See options again" returns to `/advisor/case/[ref]/options`. Choosing any action acknowledges the alert through `POST /api/bff/bookings/[id]/acknowledge` (advisor, CSRF, `Idempotency-Key`), which calls the back end's `POST /fhir/Task/{id}/$acknowledge` (created by R17) and gets back a `Parameters` with `acknowledged: true`. That cancels the shift-lead escalation. Acknowledging twice is fine. Mock it with MSW in tests using that shape.
- Withdraw reasons display: now-full "now full", staffing "staffing", cannot-reach-patient "cannot reach the patient", other "other".
- data-el: `ADV-CANCEL-CONFIRM-BTN`, `ADV-CANCEL-KEEP-BTN`, `ADV-WITHDRAWN-OPTIONS-BTN`, `ADV-WITHDRAWN-CLINICAL-BTN`, `ADV-WITHDRAWN-CALLER-BTN`.
