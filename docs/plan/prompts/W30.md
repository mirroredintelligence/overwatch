**The product.** DIUCS capacity helps an NHS urgent-care single point of access (SPOA) in Dorset. Community services, wards and virtual wards report how many referrals or beds they can take, by text message, a one-tap link, or an installed web app. Call advisors see a live wall board and a filtered list of services that fit a patient's needs, then ask a service to take the patient by text and wait for CONFIRMED. Shift leads chase late reports, register services and keep criteria current. Nothing anywhere holds a patient name or NHS number.

**The architecture.** Hexagonal. A Python back end (package `diucs` in `backend/src/diucs`) with four layers: `domain` (pure rules, no I/O), `application` (use cases and ports defined as `typing.Protocol`), `adapters` (inbound: FHIR REST, SMS webhook, link pages, workers; outbound: Neo4j, Redis, Azure Service Bus, SMS, email, push; `adapters/testing`: fakes for external providers only) and `bootstrap` (settings and the composition root). Dependencies point inwards only. A Next.js front end in `frontend/` talks only to its own `/api/bff/*` route handlers, which call the back end's FHIR R4B API. Neo4j is the only database, Redis is a cache, Azure Service Bus carries messages and scheduled work.

**Rules you need.**
- Overview: "<Service> criteria", "Version 3 · review by 12 Dec", the accepts and does-not-accept lists in plain words, the warning "Advisors rely on these. If they are out of date, advisors see a flag on your service.", buttons **Still right** and **Change**.
- Confirm: heading "Are these still right?", text "By confirming, you say the criteria are current and safe for advisors to rely on.", "Review again in" 3 months / 6 months / 12 months (one required), "We log your confirmation, the date and your role.", button **I confirm**.
- Edit: needs accepted (chips: Fall, no injury; Frailty; Wound care; IV therapy; Palliative; Dementia), youngest age (number), oldest age (number or "No limit"), needs not accepted (Suspected fracture; Head injury on anticoagulants; Needs IV therapy; Infection isolation; No exclusions — "No exclusions" clears the others), postcode districts (chips DT1 to DT4 plus add). Changed fields are labelled "Changed". A need cannot be both accepted and excluded: show "A need cannot be both accepted and not accepted". Errors use the error summary pattern.
- Preview: "What advisors will see" (service name, "Accepts: …", "Does not accept: …") and "What changed" lines such as "Youngest age 18 to 16". Diff is computed in a pure function.
- Send: "Why are you changing this?" required, hint "Short. No patient information.", max 200 characters; "Review again in" 3/6/12 months; note "A SPOA clinical lead approves it too. Until then, advisors still see version 3."
- Status ("Version 4", "Waiting for approval"): steps Sent (time, "by you"), Second approval (SPOA clinical lead), Live ("Advisors see version 4"); "We will tell you by notification. If it is sent back, you will see the note."
- BFF: `GET /api/bff/criteria/[serviceId]` → `{serviceName, current:{version, accepts[], excludes[], ageMin, ageMax|null, districts[], reviewBy}, draft?:{…, status, sentAt?, note?}}`; `POST …/confirm {reviewInMonths}`; `POST …/drafts {accepts, excludes, ageMin, ageMax, districts}`; `POST …/submit {reason, reviewInMonths}`.
- data-el: `OWN-CRIT-STILLRIGHT-BTN`, `OWN-CRIT-CHANGE-BTN`, `OWN-CRIT-CONFIRM-BTN`, `OWN-CRIT-PREVIEW-BTN`, `OWN-CRIT-SEND-BTN`.

**Build.** `app/(owner)/criteria/[serviceId]/page.tsx` with `?step=overview|confirm|edit|preview|send|status`; `components/owner/CriteriaEditor.tsx`; `lib/criteria/diff.ts` (`diffCriteria(before, after): string[]`); route handlers.
