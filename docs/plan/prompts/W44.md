You are a software engineer picking up one task in the `overwatch` repository (product name: DIUCS capacity). You have no memory of any earlier work. Everything you need is in this prompt and in the repository.

**The product.** DIUCS capacity helps an NHS urgent-care single point of access (SPOA) in Dorset. Community services, wards and virtual wards report how many referrals or beds they can take, by text message, a one-tap link, or an installed web app. Call advisors see a live wall board and a filtered list of services that fit a patient's needs, then ask a service to take the patient by text and wait for CONFIRMED. Shift leads chase late reports, register services and keep criteria current. Nothing anywhere holds a patient name or NHS number.

**The architecture.** Hexagonal. A Python back end (package `diucs` in `backend/src/diucs`) with four layers: `domain` (pure rules, no I/O), `application` (use cases and ports defined as `typing.Protocol`), `adapters` (inbound: FHIR REST, SMS webhook, link pages, workers; outbound: Neo4j, Redis, Azure Service Bus, SMS, email, push; `adapters/testing`: fakes for external providers only) and `bootstrap` (settings and the composition root). Dependencies point inwards only. A Next.js front end in `frontend/` talks only to its own `/api/bff/*` route handlers, which call the back end's FHIR R4B API. Neo4j is the only database, Redis is a cache, Azure Service Bus carries messages and scheduled work.

**Rules you need.**
- URLs under `/clinical` (`app/(clinical)/clinical/…`), because route groups add no URL segment.
- On duty `/clinical`: "Are you taking requests?" Available / Busy / Away; "1 request waiting"; "2 clinical advisors available now"; "Advisors see how many of you are available. They see your name only after you take a request." **Save status**. Choosing Away warns "Your unfinished requests go back to the queue."
- Queue `/clinical/requests`: tabs "Waiting (2)", "Mine", "Done". Each card: urgency ("Now" or "Within 15 min"), "Waiting 1 min", one summary line ("80+ · fall and frailty · NEWS2 2 · frailty 6 · borderline criteria"), **Take it**. Note: "Once you take a request, no one else can. It returns to the queue if you do not act in 10 minutes." If another clinician took it first (409): "Someone else has taken this request." and refresh. Refresh the queue every 15 seconds (fake timers in tests).
- Case `/clinical/requests/[id]`: "Case 4821", "The case" (age band, postcode, needs, urgency, referrer, scores), "Why the advisor asked", "What the advisor saw at 14:13" (one line per service: status, figure, data age), a countdown "Returns to the queue in 9 min" (live region updated each minute, not each second), **Give advice**, **Call the advisor**.
- Advice: "What do you advise?" Go ahead with <service> / Use another service / Needs a clinical call to the referrer / Send to the emergency route under local protocol (required); "Why? (optional)" Within criteria on my judgement / Safer alternative / Other; optional note, max 280 characters with a counter and the warning "Add a short note only if needed. No patient names."; **Send advice**.
- Sent: "Advice sent", "The advisor has it now. Logged for audit: your role, the time, your advice and what you saw.", "Clinical overrides are reviewed with the service leads, not used to assess advisors.", **Next request**, **Go off duty**.
- Notifications for clinical advisors carry urgency and age band only; this task does not send them.
- BFF: `PUT /api/bff/clinical/presence {presence}`; `GET /api/bff/clinical/summary` → `{waiting, available}`; `GET /api/bff/clinical/requests?tab=waiting|mine|done`; `POST /api/bff/clinical/requests/[id]/take` (409 if taken); `GET /api/bff/clinical/requests/[id]` → `{reference, case:{ageBand, postcode, needs[], urgency, referrer, scores[]}, reason, shown:[{service, status, value, age}], shownAt, takenAt, returnsAt}`; `POST /api/bff/clinical/requests/[id]/advice {advice, reason?, note?}`.
