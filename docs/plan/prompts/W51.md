**The product.** DIUCS capacity helps an NHS urgent-care single point of access (SPOA) in Dorset. Community services, wards and virtual wards report how many referrals or beds they can take, by text message, a one-tap link, or an installed web app. Call advisors see a live wall board and a filtered list of services that fit a patient's needs, then ask a service to take the patient by text and wait for CONFIRMED. Shift leads chase late reports, register services and keep criteria current. Nothing anywhere holds a patient name or NHS number.

**The architecture.** Hexagonal. A Python back end (package `diucs` in `backend/src/diucs`) with four layers: `domain` (pure rules, no I/O), `application` (use cases and ports defined as `typing.Protocol`), `adapters` (inbound: FHIR REST, SMS webhook, link pages, workers; outbound: Neo4j, Redis, Azure Service Bus, SMS, email, push; `adapters/testing`: fakes for external providers only) and `bootstrap` (settings and the composition root). Dependencies point inwards only. A Next.js front end in `frontend/` talks only to its own `/api/bff/*` route handlers, which call the back end's FHIR R4B API. Neo4j is the only database, Redis is a cache, Azure Service Bus carries messages and scheduled work.

**Rules you need.**
- URL `/planning`. Header "DIUCS capacity · Planning", "Managers and ICB · sign in with Trust account". Heading "Right-sizing: where capacity ran out".
- Filters: Period (Last 30 days / Last 90 days / Last 12 months), Need group (All / Falls and frailty / IV therapy / Palliative). Cohort line from the API, for example "Cohort: age 65+, falls and frailty, within 2 hrs, ambulance crew or care home referrers. Groups under 10 are hidden."
- Headline figures: "640 referrals", "112 best fit unavailable (18% of referrals. Full, closed or not updated.)", "62 went to another suitable service".
- "Where they went when the best fit was full": table Best fit and its state / Went to / Referrals / Share, with a bar per row. Suppressed rows read "fewer than 10" and show no share or bar. "Counts under 10 are not shown, to protect small groups."
- "When the best fit was unavailable": a day-by-time-of-day grid (Mon to Sun × 00 to 06, 06 to 12, 12 to 18, 18 to 24), shading by count plus the number in each cell, and "Busiest: Thursday 12:00 to 18:00 (9)". A data table is the accessible form.
- "Declined despite showing capacity": by service, with "For a conversation with each service. Not a league table and not used to assess advisors." Never show advisors or people.
- "How to read this": "These are referral and acceptance counts from the SPOA decision log. They do not show what happened to patients afterwards. Outcomes need linked data and come in phase 2."
- **Export table (CSV)** and **Show cohort definition** (a disclosure).
- BFF: `GET /api/bff/planning?period=90d&need=falls-frailty` → `{cohort, referrals, bestFitUnavailable:{count, pct}, wentElsewhere, flows:[{bestFit, state, wentTo, count|null, suppressed}], grid:[[n…]×4]×7, busiest:{day, band, count}, declinedDespite:[{service, count|null, suppressed}]}`; `GET /api/bff/planning/export?…` → CSV.
